Smart-Clearanceहर कार्टन को दूसरा मौका

Every near-expiry carton gets a second chance, chosen by AI. An agent that decides, batch by batch, where short-dated stock earns the most in the days it has left, then goes and does it.

Client: Munchly Foods (fictional) Marketplace: ExpireSoon (generic Indian B2B marketplace, mocked) Demo: full 9 stages · about 6 min PWA with in-app push · WhatsApp = future scope Highlights: ₹ recovered · GST ITC · BRSR Out of scope: medicines
Name, decided by the team
Smart-Clearance

Says what it does in two words, and reads the same to judges, finance and a distributor. "Short-Date Router" stays as the internal name of the routing engine inside it.

Tagline: "Every near-expiry carton gets a second chance, chosen by AI." Hindi line for the notification voice: हर कार्टन को दूसरा मौका.

The client we are prototyping for

One fictional B2B client, built in enough detail that every number in the demo has a source. Munchly Foods is a mid-size listed snacks company, so GST, BRSR and FSSAI all apply to it.

Munchly Foods LtdPune. Listed on NSE and among the top 1,000 listed companies by market cap, so BRSR applies. GSTIN 27AABCM1234F1Z5. FSSAI licence 10019022001234. ₹640 crore revenue, 8 demo SKUs: Masala Chips, Choco Cream Biscuits, Peanut Chikki, Instant Poha, Masala Oats, Mango Drink, plus a personal-care line (Glowra Face Wash, Coconut Hair Oil).
Distributor network4 super-stockists in the demo: Rakesh Traders (Nagpur), Patil Distributors (Pune), Gupta & Sons (Indore), Lakshmi Agencies (Hyderabad). 195 kiranas in their clusters. Expiry policyDistributors buy this pack from Munchly at ₹22. Munchly takes back expired stock at full credit and destroys it, so a distributor's short-dated stock ends up as Munchly's loss. Left alone, a distributor waits for the claim instead of selling at a loss, which is why Munchly, not the distributor, runs Smart-Clearance. The distributor still says yes: he gets his money within days instead of a claim settled weeks after expiry, with no claim paperwork.
The problem, in numbersQuick-commerce apps reject stock below their shelf-life gate (Blinkit 90+ days; Zepto and Instamart 60% of life). Because Munchly's distributors supply those warehouses, short-dated stock stays in the distributor's godown. Munchly's distributors are left holding about ₹18 lakh of short-dated stock per quarter, which comes back to Munchly as expiry claims. Today 70% of it is destroyed, which also reverses the GST input credit on it.

How Munchly's stock reaches the shelf

Munchly sells only to its distributors. Each distributor supplies two kinds of customer: the kiranas in his beat, and the Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart warehouses in his city. Quick commerce turns away short-dated stock at inbound, so a batch that misses the gate stays in the distributor's godown.

Why the batch is stuck. Rakesh Traders normally sends part of every batch to the Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart warehouses in Nagpur, and anything they reject at inbound comes back to his godown. With 47 days left this batch cannot go there at all, and his kiranas sell only 12 units a day. Smart-Clearance steps in at the godown and routes the 1,360 units that will not sell in time. Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart are never a destination in the plan.

Stage by stage, with the demo batch

The demo follows one batch end to end: MF-2409-117, Munchly Masala Chips 150 g, 1,840 units at Rakesh Traders' godown in Nagpur, best-before in 47 days, MRP ₹30, selling 12 units a day in that cluster. Day 0 is 2 Oct 2026, the Watcher's first run after Munchly onboards.

1CONNECT

Priya connects the data and sets the guardrails

Priya · Anita · Data Agent · once, 15 minutes

Priya uploads the distributor stock export (batch, best-before, units, godown) and the last 90 days of secondary sales. The Data Agent maps the columns and loads BigQuery. Anita sets the money rules the Router must obey.

  • Channel allow-list per category (personal care never goes to food banks; premium gift packs never to staff sale).
  • Floor price: never below 35% of MRP for snacks and biscuits, 40% for staples, 30% for beverages.
  • Donation partners: Feeding India and India FoodBanking Network, both with their intake rules.
  • Territory guard: ExpireSoon listings are hidden from buyers inside Munchly's distributor territories, matched by pincode (Nagpur with Wardha and Kamptee, Pune, Indore, Hyderabad), so clearance stock never undercuts Munchly's own distributors.
  • Distributor permission: Rakesh Traders gives a one-time permission in the app. Within Munchly's price floors, Smart-Clearance may, in his name, list his Munchly stock on ExpireSoon, send scheme offers to his kiranas, draft his invoices and book dispatch slots in his calendar. Everything shows in his app, he can pause any of it, and he gets a push at approval and when a buyer wins.
  • Return window for scheme stock: kiranas can return unsold scheme packs to the distributor's salesman until 20 days before best-before. Returns reach the godown with at least 20 days left, in time for the Nagpur staff sale or Feeding India (15+ days), never as dead stock. IFBN needs 21+ days, so it does not take returns.
  • Planning assumptions, editable: the 3.5× scheme uplift and the ₹0.50 van rate.
  • Approval policy: first 10 routes per channel need a tap; after that the agent runs within guardrails and reports.
Shown on screenThe write-off formula: inventory at cost + ITC reversal + disposal + EPR. Anita sees it before any batch is routed.

2DETECT

The Watcher raises a batch that will not make it

Watcher Agent · day 0, 09:00 · Pub/Sub batch.at_risk

Every morning the Watcher runs every batch through the three quick-commerce gates and projects sell-through to the best-before date. Retailers will not take stock in the last week, so the projection stops seven days before expiry.

  • Blinkit needs 90+ days: batch has 47. Rakesh can no longer ship it to the Blinkit warehouse in Nagpur.
  • Zepto and Instamart need 60% of a 184-day life (110 days). Blocked there too.
  • Only his kiranas are left: 12 a day × 40 usable days = 480 units will sell. 1,360 units at risk, ₹40,800 at MRP.

3VERIFY

Rakesh bhai sends one photo; Gemini reads the carton

Rakesh bhai taps the notification and photographs the carton label with the app's camera. 5DECIDE

The Router proposes a split, not a single channel

Router Agent · day 0, 09:22

The Router fills the best-paying channel to its capacity, then the next, until the at-risk units are placed. Anything that cannot be placed before expiry goes to a food bank; only after that, write-off. It writes its reasoning in plain language and shows one alternative so Priya can disagree with something concrete.

  • 588 units → kirana cluster at ₹18 effective (₹21.60 a pack, 2 free with every 10): the best price, and it keeps stock inside Munchly's own trade. Capped by what 38 kiranas can move in 14 days with a buy-10-get-2 scheme, as extra sales on top of their normal 12 a day (already counted in the 480).
  • 772 units → ExpireSoon at ₹15 (reserve ₹13.50): unlimited depth, 5–9 days, buyer pays freight.
  • The Nagpur staff sale is eligible but pays less per unit than ExpireSoon, so it gets nothing this time.
  • Alternative shown: all 1,360 to ExpireSoon, net ₹20,300. ₹1,470 less, and nothing stays in own trade.

7EXECUTE

Four agents go to work, in parallel

Lister · Outreach · Negotiator · Donation · day 0 to 14 (shelf check on day 7)
  • Lister builds the ExpireSoon listing (title, batch, best-before, MRP ₹30, offer ₹15, 772 units, Nagpur, ambient storage, the label photo, invoice available) under Rakesh Traders' seller account, using his one-time permission and visible only to buyers outside Munchly's distributor territories, and calls POST /v1/listings. The listing also shows in Rakesh's app. In the prototype that call is mocked; the response is a listing ID and a URL that opens the ExpireSoon page.
  • Outreach picks the 38 kiranas with the best 30-day sell-through for this SKU and sends a Hindi push notification with the scheme that opens an in-app offer page with a quantity stepper. Each shop's order is capped at 4× its own 14-day sales, a ceiling a little above the 3.5× plan, so no single kirana is overloaded. Orders flow to Rakesh bhai's van route. 31 of the 38 kiranas order 588 units between them, each under its cap, in about two hours.
  • Outreach, day 7: shelf check. On his beat, Rakesh's salesman counts the scheme packs left at each kirana and enters the count in his order app. The agent compares each shop's pace with the days left. Where a shop is not selling, it recommends bringing the packs back to Rakesh's godown on the salesman's next round, with no extra trip, for the Nagpur staff sale or a Feeding India donation (pooled at the godown to the 50-unit pickup minimum). Example: a Kamptee shop took 24 packs and still has 18, so it is selling one a day; the agent recommends picking up 12 on Thursday's round and leaving the 6 it can sell in time.
  • Negotiator watches the listing. Agrawal Wholesale in Raipur bids ₹13.00 for all 772 (below the ₹13.50 reserve). The agent counters at ₹14.20 with a 24-hour dispatch promise taken from Rakesh bhai's calendar. Buyer accepts, pays the 15% token (₹1,644), balance due in 48 hours. Rakesh gets a push that the buyer has won.
  • Donation is idle for this batch. For the Mango Drink batch, the demo's donation example (22 days left), it books Feeding India in Hyderabad, because that batch's donation remainder is 58 units, below IFBN's 100-unit minimum.

8SETTLE

The Paperwork Agent closes the loop for finance

Paperwork Agent · Anita · Rakesh bhai · day 3 to 7
  • Tax invoice from Rakesh Traders to the Raipur buyer, drafted for him to issue from Tally: 772 × ₹14.20 = ₹10,962 taxable, IGST 5% = ₹548 (Maharashtra → Chhattisgarh; chips moved from 12% to 5% under GST 2.0 on 22 Sep 2025). MRP stays ₹30 on the pack; a discounted sale is fine, a second MRP is not (Legal Metrology rules).
  • E-way bill check: consignment ₹11,510 incl. GST is below the ₹50,000 threshold, so none is needed. The agent re-checks if dispatch is split.
  • Price-support credit note (financial, no GST adjustment) from Munchly to Rakesh Traders: the gap between his ₹22 purchase price and what each channel fetched (₹7.80 a unit on the 772 sold on ExpireSoon, ₹4 on the 588 sold to kiranas, ₹8,374), plus the ₹294 van and ₹100 listing fee he paid, ₹8,768 in all. This also covers the buy-10-get-2 scheme: kiranas pay ₹21.60 a pack and get 2 free with every 10, so ₹18 is already the effective price and no separate scheme credit note is needed. Rakesh ends whole, and Munchly avoids an expiry claim of ₹34,490 (₹29,920 credit for 1,360 × ₹22, plus disposal, EPR and ITC reversal).
  • GST ITC memo: goods were supplied under tax invoices, so Section 17(5)(h) reversal does not apply. ₹1,224 of Munchly's input credit stays (₹0.90 of input GST in each pack, from the cost sheet). It would be reversed only if the stock came back under the expiry claim and Munchly destroyed it. ITC on any donated units is reversed: s.17(5)(h) blocks credit on gifts, and since 1 Oct 2023 s.17(5)(fa) blocks it for CSR donations too. Reported in GSTR-3B Table 4(B)(1).
  • FSSAI surplus-food checklist and partner receipt: not required for this batch; generated for the Mango Drink donation example.
  • True-up after 29 Oct: when the return window closes, returned packs go to the Nagpur staff sale or Feeding India, and the price support and the ledger are corrected.
  • Destruction certificate only if units remain, with the ITC reversal entry pre-filled so finance is never surprised.

9REPORT

Finance and sustainability get the same ledger

Impact Agent · Vikram · Anita · CFO · monthly and quarterly

Every routed batch lands in one ledger with both readings of the same event: the money reading (₹ recovered, write-off avoided, ITC protected, disposal and EPR avoided) and the circularity reading (kg diverted, CO₂e, meals). The BRSR Core Principle 6 table exports with the evidence links: invoices, listing IDs, in-app order logs, food-bank receipts.

  • Headline tiles: ₹ recovered this month, swing vs write-off, ITC protected, kg diverted, CO₂e, meals.
  • Trend: weekly recovered vs would-be write-off, one axis, two series.
  • Channel mix: where the units went, so Priya can see if one channel is being over-used.
  • "How the tax maths works" panel: the Section 17(5)(h) and 17(5)(fa) rules in one paragraph, with the indicative flags.

Channels

Four places a carton can go, plus the baseline it is measured against. The rules below are the ones the Valuer uses in the prototype.

ExpireSoon Indian B2B near-expiry marketplace

30+ days left · 45–55% of MRP · 5–9 days · unlimited depth
  • Seller flow: business account → product, batch, labelled date, location → quantity and offer → publish for buyer review.
  • Buyers bid; a bid carries a 15% token, balance within 48 hours; in-app chat with the seller.
  • First 5 listings free, then ₹100 each (Rakesh has used his free ones). Buyer pays freight.
  • The seller is the distributor who owns the stock; Smart-Clearance lists in his name under his one-time permission. Listings are hidden from buyers inside Munchly's distributor territories.
  • Prototype: our own marketplace UI and a mocked POST /v1/listings. Production: a partner API with a real marketplace, or a Gemini computer-use agent on its web form.

Kirana cluster push

20+ days · 60% of MRP · 10–14 days · capacity = cluster sell-through × 14 days × 3.5 (extra sales on top of normal)
  • The distributor's own kiranas, ranked by 30-day sell-through for the SKU.
  • Each shop's order is capped at 4× its own 14-day sales.
  • Hindi / Marathi / Telugu push notification with a scheme (buy 10 get 2), a 48-hour window and an in-app order page.
  • Kiranas pay ₹21.60 a pack and get 2 free with every 10, so ₹18 effective. Van delivery ₹0.50 per unit. Munchly's price-support credit note covers the free goods.

Nagpur staff sale

until best-before · 40% of MRP · 2–3 days · cap 50 units
  • At Rakesh Traders' godown, for his staff and Munchly's Nagpur sales team. Rakesh is the seller: buyers pay to his UPI QR and collect from the godown.
  • Fast and cheap; capped so it never becomes the default.

Write-off baseline

−(cost + ITC reversal + disposal + EPR) per unit
  • CGST s.17(5)(h) blocks input credit on goods destroyed, written off or given as gifts; CBIC Circular 72/46/2018 covers time-expired medicines; the same logic applies here by analogy.
  • Disposal ₹1.50 per unit and EPR / waste liability ₹6 per kg disposed (160 g a unit, product and pack) are indicative factors; both are visible and editable in setup.

Clearing costs less to move than destroying. Sending 588 units to kiranas costs ₹294 in van trips and the ExpireSoon buyer pays his own freight. Trucking all 1,360 units to be destroyed would cost ₹2,040. Both figures are already in the ledger: the van in the ₹394 cost line, the disposal truck in the ₹2,040 disposal line.

Indicative labels. Three figures are indicative, and the prototype says so on screen: the disposal rate, the EPR rupee factor and the CO₂e factor. The 3.5× scheme uplift and the ₹0.50 van rate are planning assumptions, editable in setup. Every other number is either a published rule (q-commerce gates, GST rates, ITC reversal on destroyed and donated goods, e-way bill threshold, Legal Metrology) or the client's own data (cost sheet, sell-through).

Why finance cares more than supply chain. Supply chain sees ₹40,800 of chips. Finance sees a ₹26,330 hit to the P&L, ₹4,570 of it in cash and credit, which the write-off hides as a stock adjustment. Smart-Clearance puts both on the same screen.

Who keeps what. Rakesh Traders owns the stock and collects the sale proceeds from the buyers. Munchly pays him a price-support credit note so he ends whole at his ₹22 purchase price: ₹8,150 at plan prices (₹7,756 price gap + ₹394 van and listing fee), ₹8,768 after negotiation. Munchly's own view: it pays ₹8,150 instead of an expiry claim of ₹34,490 (₹29,920 credit + ₹2,040 disposal + ₹1,306 EPR + ₹1,224 ITC reversal), the same ₹26,340 swing as the ledger above, which shows the batch as one system at Munchly's ₹16 cost.
