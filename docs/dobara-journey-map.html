Smart-Clearanceहर कार्टन को दूसरा मौका

Every near-expiry carton gets a second chance, chosen by AI. An agent that decides, batch by batch, where short-dated stock earns the most in the days it has left, then goes and does it.

Client: Munchly Foods (fictional) Marketplace: ExpireSoon (generic Indian B2B marketplace, mocked) Demo: full 9 stages · about 6 min PWA with in-app push · WhatsApp = future scope Highlights: ₹ recovered · GST ITC · BRSR Out of scope: medicines
Name, decided by the team
Smart-Clearance

Says what it does in two words, and reads the same to judges, finance and a distributor. "Short-Date Router" stays as the internal name of the routing engine inside it.

Tagline: "Every near-expiry carton gets a second chance, chosen by AI." Hindi line for the notification voice: हर कार्टन को दूसरा मौका.

Stage by stage, with the demo batch

The demo follows one batch end to end: MF-2409-117, Munchly Masala Chips 150 g, 1,840 units at Rakesh Traders' godown in Nagpur, best-before in 47 days, MRP ₹30, selling 12 units a day in that cluster.

1CONNECT

Priya connects the data and sets the guardrails

Priya · Anita · Data Agent · once, 15 minutes

Priya uploads the distributor stock export (batch, best-before, units, godown) and the last 90 days of secondary sales. The Data Agent maps the columns and loads BigQuery. Anita sets the money rules the Router must obey.

  • Channel allow-list per category (personal care never goes to food banks; premium gift packs never to staff sale).
  • Floor price: never below 35% of MRP for snacks and biscuits, 40% for staples, 30% for beverages.
  • Donation partners: Feeding India and India FoodBanking Network, both with their intake rules.
  • Approval policy: first 10 routes per channel need a tap; after that the agent runs within guardrails and reports.
Shown on screenThe write-off formula: inventory at cost + ITC reversal + disposal + EPR. Anita sees it before any batch is routed.
PrototypeS0 Setup, static page with the rules pre-filled.
2DETECT

The Watcher raises a batch that will not make it

Watcher Agent · day 0, 09:00 · Pub/Sub batch.at_risk

Every morning the Watcher runs every batch through the three quick-commerce gates and projects sell-through to the best-before date. Retailers will not take stock in the last week, so the projection stops seven days before expiry.

  • Blinkit needs 90+ days: batch has 47. Blocked.
  • Zepto and Instamart need 60% of a 365-day life (219 days). Blocked.
  • Sell-through 12 a day × 40 usable days = 480 units will sell. 1,360 units at risk, ₹40,800 at MRP.

3VERIFY

Rakesh bhai sends one photo; Gemini reads the carton

Vision Agent · Rakesh bhai · day 0, 09:20

Distributor stock data in India is often stale, so before any money is quoted the agent asks for the truth from the godown floor. Rakesh bhai taps the notification and photographs the carton label with the app's camera. Gemini extracts batch number, MFG date, best-before and MRP, with a confidence score, and reconciles it with the DMS record.

  • Batch MF-2409-117 · MFG 18 May 2026 · Best before 18 Nov 2026 · MRP ₹30.00 · confidence 0.97. Matches.
  • If it did not match, the agent would re-run the risk on the photo's date and flag the DMS row for correction.
  • The photo becomes the listing image and the audit evidence for the BRSR line.

5DECIDE

The Router proposes a split, not a single channel

Router Agent · day 0, 09:22

The Router fills the best-paying channel to its capacity, then the next, until the at-risk units are placed. Anything that cannot be placed before expiry goes to a food bank; only after that, write-off. It writes its reasoning in plain language and shows one alternative so Priya can disagree with something concrete.

  • 588 units → kirana cluster at ₹18: the best price, and it keeps stock inside Munchly's own trade. Capped by what 38 kiranas can move in 14 days with a buy-10-get-2 scheme.
  • 772 units → ExpireSoon at ₹15 (reserve ₹13.50): unlimited depth, 5–9 days, buyer pays freight.
  • D2C and staff sale are eligible but pay less per unit than ExpireSoon, so they get nothing this time.
  • Alternative shown: all 1,360 to ExpireSoon, net ₹20,300. ₹1,470 less, and nothing stays in own trade.

7EXECUTE

Four agents go to work, in parallel

Lister · Outreach · Negotiator · Donation · day 0 to day 3
  • Lister builds the ExpireSoon listing (title, batch, best-before, MRP ₹30, offer ₹15, 772 units, Nagpur, ambient storage, the label photo, invoice available) and calls POST /v1/listings. In the prototype that call is mocked; the response is a listing ID and a URL that opens the ExpireSoon page.
  • Outreach picks the 38 kiranas with the best 30-day sell-through for this SKU and sends a Hindi push notification with the scheme that opens an in-app offer page with a quantity stepper. Orders flow to Rakesh bhai's van route. 14 kiranas order 588 units within about two hours.
  • Negotiator watches the listing. Sri Venkateswara Traders bids ₹13.00 for all 772 (below the ₹13.50 reserve). The agent counters at ₹14.20 with a 24-hour dispatch promise taken from Rakesh bhai's calendar. Buyer accepts, pays the 15% token (₹1,644), balance due in 48 hours.
  • Donation is idle for this batch. For the Mango Drink batch (22 days left) it books Feeding India in Hyderabad, because IFBN needs 21+ days and 100+ units and that batch's donation remainder is 58 units.

8SETTLE

The Paperwork Agent closes the loop for finance

Paperwork Agent · Anita · Rakesh bhai · day 3 to 7

  • E-way bill check: consignment ₹12,278 incl. GST is below the ₹50,000 threshold, so none is needed. The agent re-checks if dispatch is split.
  • Credit note from Munchly to Rakesh Traders for the buy-10-get-2 scheme: 98 free units at cost, ₹1,568.
  • GST ITC memo: goods were supplied under tax invoices, so Section 17(5)(h) reversal does not apply. ₹2,611 of input credit stays. Any donated units are shown as reversed, labelled indicative.
  • FSSAI surplus-food checklist and partner receipt for any donated units (Mango Drink batch).
  • Destruction certificate only if units remain, with the ITC reversal entry pre-filled so finance is never surprised.

Channels

Five places a carton can go, plus the baseline it is measured against. The rules below are the ones the Valuer uses in the prototype.

ExpireSoon Indian B2B near-expiry marketplace

30+ days left · 45–55% of MRP · 5–9 days · unlimited depth
  • Seller flow: business account → product, batch, labelled date, location → quantity and offer → publish for buyer review.
  • Buyers bid; a bid carries a 15% token, balance within 48 hours; in-app chat with the seller.
  • First 5 listings free, then ₹100 each. Buyer pays freight.
  • Prototype: our own marketplace UI and a mocked POST /v1/listings. Production: a partner API with a real marketplace, or a Gemini computer-use agent on its web form.

Kirana cluster push

20+ days · 60% of MRP · 10–14 days · capacity = cluster sell-through × 14 days × 3.5 (scheme uplift)
  • The distributor's own kiranas, ranked by 30-day sell-through for the SKU.
  • Hindi / Marathi / Telugu push notification with a scheme (buy 10 get 2), a 48-hour window and an in-app order page.
  • Van delivery ₹0.50 per unit. Brand issues a credit note for the free goods.

Discount D2C

25+ days · 50% of MRP · 7–12 days · capacity 8% of batch
  • Clearance page on the brand's site plus GoFig-style partners (Pune, Mumbai).
  • Courier ₹3 per unit, so it rarely wins on net ₹; included so the Router can show why not.

Staff sale

any days · 40% of MRP · 2–3 days · cap 150 units
  • HR notice with a UPI QR at the Pune plant and the Nagpur godown.
  • Fast and cheap; capped so it never becomes the default.

Food banks both supported

food only · ₹0 · 1–2 days · FSSAI surplus-food regulations 2019
Intake ruleFeeding India (Zomato)India FoodBanking Network
Minimum days to best-before at hand-over1521
Minimum quantity per pickup50 units100 units or 1 pallet
LogisticsVolunteer pickup within 48 h, hunger spots in 100+ citiesDrop at member food-bank warehouse (Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata) or scheduled pickup
PaperIn-app receiptDonation acknowledgement (CSR / 80G, indicative)
  • The Donation Agent picks the partner whose rules the batch meets and whose hub is nearest; large lots can be split across both.
  • ITC on donated units is shown as reversed and labelled indicative (team decision).

Write-off baseline

−(cost + ITC reversal + disposal + EPR) per unit
  • CGST s.17(5)(h) blocks input credit on goods destroyed, written off or given as gifts; CBIC Circular 72/46/2018 covers time-expired goods.
  • Disposal ₹1.50 per unit and EPR ₹6 per kg of packaging are indicative factors; both are visible and editable in setup.

Indicative labels. Three figures are indicative, and the prototype says so on screen: the ITC treatment of donated units (s.17(5)(h) blocks credit on gifts; a CSR-route donation may differ), the EPR rupee factor, and the CO₂e factor. Every other number is either a published rule (q-commerce gates, GST rates, e-way bill threshold, Legal Metrology) or the client's own data.

Why finance cares more than supply chain. Supply chain sees ₹40,800 of chips. Finance sees ₹27,717 of cash and credit leaving the business, which the write-off hides as a stock adjustment. Smart-Clearance puts both on the same screen.
