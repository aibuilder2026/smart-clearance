Smart-Clearance · the story of one carton · for anyone

One carton of chips, nine scenes, a second chanceहर कार्टन को दूसरा मौका

Munchly Foods makes snacks in Pune. In its Nagpur warehouse sit 1,840 packets of Masala Chips with 47 days left before the best-before date. The delivery apps will not take them any more. Without help they go in the bin. This is what happens instead.

When stock is destroyed, the company must return the GST credit it claimed on it. When stock is sold, even cheaply, the credit stays. The agent writes the invoice, checks whether an e-way bill is needed, issues the credit note for the shop scheme, and files the memo that proves the credit was kept.
