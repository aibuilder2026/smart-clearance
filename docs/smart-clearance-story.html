How Munchly's stock reaches a shopper, and where it gets stuck

Munchly never sells to a shop or an app directly. Every packet goes to a distributor first, at ₹22 plus 5% GST, and the distributor feeds two lanes: the kiranas his salesman visits each week, and the quick-commerce warehouses in his city. The second lane checks shelf life at the door. When a batch fails that check it stays in the godown, and if it does not sell in time it travels back up the chain to Munchly.

The carton at the centre of the story

Rakesh Traders ordered this batch for the festive season and planned to send part of it to the quick-commerce warehouses in Nagpur. By the time it was ready to ship, the batch had crossed their shelf-life lines, so all 77 cartons (76 full, one with 16 packets) are still in his godown at Kalamna Market, selling to local shops at about 12 packets a day.
