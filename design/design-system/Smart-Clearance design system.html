Operate surfaces for people in a hurry: a supply-chain manager on her phone at 09:00, a distributor in a godown, a shopkeeper at his counter. Paper-light ground, quiet white cards, colour reserved for meaning. Read readme.md for the rules; this page shows the tokens at real size.

Colour

Restrained: neutrals plus one accent. Four more hues are reserved for meaning and never decorate. Text on a tinted ground takes the ink of that hue, never grey.

Where each hue lives

  • Green: the agent, verified, the primary button, done steps.
  • Amber: approve, the current step, focus rings, the demo bar.
  • Red: at-risk zone, losses, the bin door, unread badges.
  • Purple: ExpireSoon header, buttons, bubbles. Blue: push cards and the Hindi offer banner.

Motion conveys state, never decoration. One authored moment per screen; everything else is 150 to 350 ms with an exponential ease-out. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Layout

The app is a size container; layouts switch on its own width so a device frame and a real viewport behave the same. Phone is the default: bottom navigation of at most five items, one column, the primary action at thumb height. From 760 px a 96 px rail replaces the bottom nav and secondary panels sit beside the main column; from 1100 px the board shows all five zones side by side.

Targets and sizes

  • Hit targets 44 px; inputs 48 px; bottom nav 64 px.
  • Body 16 px on phones; never under 13 px for functional text.
  • Cards never nest. Zones, chips and bars carry colour; cards stay white.

Chrome

  • Top bar: who is signed in, a bell with the unread count, a back control on detail screens.
  • Rail: icon + label, the agent mark on top, the person at the bottom.
  • Sheets, scrims and push cards position inside the app container, not the window.
