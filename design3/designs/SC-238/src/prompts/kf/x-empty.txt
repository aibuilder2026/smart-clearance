A medium shot at the edge of a freshly dug landfill pit of grey-brown earth on an overcast morning. A worker in his thirties in an orange high-visibility vest, a cloth dust mask and grey work gloves crouches at the pit's edge holding a plain brown cardboard carton slit open along its top, tipping it so that small plain red foil snack packets slide out of it into the pit, where many more red packets already lie scattered on the muddy earth. Behind him, softly out of focus, a second worker in an orange vest carries another opened carton, and a yellow backhoe loader stands beyond under a pale grey sky. The packets are plain red with no printing. Muted, true-to-life documentary colours, soft diffused light, a real place.
