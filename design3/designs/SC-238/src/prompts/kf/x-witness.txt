A medium shot of two independent witnesses standing side by side at a landfill on an overcast morning: an Indian woman in her forties in a beige cotton salwar kameez with a dupatta over one shoulder, and an Indian man in his thirties in a plain white shirt with the sleeves rolled up. The woman holds a grey tablet at chest height with its back towards the camera, tilted towards herself, and looks down at its screen, her right index finger resting on its top edge, about to sign; the man stands at her shoulder, looking down at the tablet, waiting his turn. The muddy pit and a yellow backhoe loader are softly out of focus behind them. Muted, true-to-life documentary colours, soft diffused light.
