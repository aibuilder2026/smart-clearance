A medium-wide shot into a freshly dug landfill pit of grey-brown earth on an overcast morning: small plain red foil snack packets lie scattered across the floor of the pit, and the toothed steel bucket of a yellow backhoe loader enters from the right, low over the ground, pushing a rolling wave of dark grey-brown earth across the pit and over the red packets, burying them; a little dust lifts. The machine's yellow arm and the bucket are clean and simple, the cab out of frame. Muted, true-to-life documentary colours, soft diffused light, a real place.
