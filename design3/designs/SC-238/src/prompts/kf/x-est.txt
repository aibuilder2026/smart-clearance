A wide establishing shot of an authorised municipal sanitary landfill on the edge of an Indian city on an overcast morning. In the foreground a freshly dug pit of grey-brown earth, its sides cut clean; a yellow backhoe loader stands beside the pit; a small plain white tipper truck with no markings is backed up to the far edge of the pit; low heaps of covered waste and soil stretch behind, a line of trees far off, and a pale grey sky with a few black kites circling. Three small figures stand at the pit's edge: two workers in orange high-visibility vests and an older man in a light blue checked shirt holding a tablet. Muted, desaturated, true-to-life documentary colours, soft diffused light, wet ground, a real place.
