Part 2 · The swing, explained

Today

What the swing is (money.js, for the Mango Drink)

If it were destroyed

1,580 packs: the stock written off at cost ₹17,380.00, the GST input credit reversed ₹869.00, disposal ₹2,370.00 and EPR ₹2,038.20. A P&L of −₹22,657.20, ₹14.34 a pack.

With the plan

The kiranas ₹15,778.00, the staff sale ₹1,200.00, the food bank −₹60.90: ₹16,917.10 recovered. The stock leaves the books at cost either way (−₹17,380.00), so a P&L of −₹462.90.

The swing

−₹462.90 − (−₹22,657.20) = ₹22,194.30. Put another way: the ₹16,917.10 recovered plus the ₹5,277.20 destroying would have cost beyond the stock. Every option computes it from money.js, for whichever batch the card shows.

Option A · How it adds up

Recommended

Under the figure a note says the swing in words, “₹22,194 better than destroying it · How it adds up”, and opens the working in a sheet: the swing, a bridge from the P&L if destroyed to the P&L with the plan (a step for each exit and one for what was not paid), the bin's cost line by line, what each exit did, the swing as a sum, and how the loss was kept small (the best-paying exit first, the exits left out and why, the GST credit kept, no disposal or EPR).

Trade-offs
  • Every figure on the card can be followed to its rule, in one place, and the card stays as it is.
  • The longest read; the bridge needs its caption to say what “Not paid” is.
  • The build: a sheet and one chart in core, from figures money.js already gives (and the live case already carries).

Option B · Beside the figure

Under the figure, Show the working opens two columns in place, on the card itself: destroyed against the plan, line by line (sold or given, van and fees, the GST credit, disposal, EPR, the stock at cost), each P&L at its foot and the swing as the difference. No sheet and no chart.

Trade-offs
  • The reader never leaves the card, and the two columns make the comparison plain.
  • The card grows by half while it is open; on a phone the figures sit tight.
  • Says what the swing is, less why each exit was chosen.

Option C · Pack by pack

An ⓘ beside “swing” opens where the swing came from: one bar of the swing split by exit, each exit's share (the kirana scheme ₹20,360.48, 92%) and what a pack sent there was worth against the bin, then each exit's pack beside the bin's on one scale (₹11.50, ₹8.00, −₹1.05 against −₹14.34). A popover on desktops and tablets, a sheet on phones.

Trade-offs
  • Answers “why so much” by the exits, quickly, and compact.
  • The bin's own lines are one sentence, and the P&L is not shown.
  • A pack's share adds what destroying it would have cost beyond its book cost (₹3.34), which needs its sentence.
