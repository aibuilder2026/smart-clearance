A wide establishing shot of an Indian distributor's godown yard at dawn, from a slightly raised camera at the gate: a long corrugated-roof godown with its roller door open and racks of plain kraft-brown cartons with a sage-green band inside, a small first-floor office with a window above it, a concrete yard with a weighing scale and a handcart, a tarpaulin folded by the gate, a chai stall at the wall, a neem tree at the right. Low warm sun, long shadows, light mist. A small blue truck has just arrived, and the brand's supply planner (a woman in a navy pinstripe kurta) and the distributor (a man in his fifties in a light blue checked shirt) shake hands beside it while two workers in grey T-shirts unload cartons. Cinematic 35 mm, deep focus, everything sharp.
