Recovered and destroyed

SC-149 · the console's Overview · three ways for its Recovered figure and the Recovered, by day card to stop drawing the same thing twice, and to show the food destroyed. Agents at work, the other figures, the live batches and the side lists stay exactly as they are.

The maintainer (10 Oct):

“Console dashboard graph across all clients seems to be duplicating the recovered graph, see if we can add destroyed food into the graphs too... Agents at work and other sections looks fine dont touch them”

What the Overview shows today

The first figure, Recovered, 30 days, and its sparkline draw the recovered series; the card below draws the same series again, under the same total. Nothing on the Overview says what was destroyed: the batches table keeps what a closed batch recovered, not the packs no channel took.

In every option

Nothing repeats

The money is shown once as a series; the card answers a second question: what happened to the food.

Destroyed, measured

A closed batch keeps the packs it destroyed (at the godown or by the client), so every figure is an aggregate over the batches, as the rest of the dashboard is. Kilos come from each SKU's kilos a pack.

Read without colour

Kept out of landfill in the console's green, destroyed in clay with a hatch, below the baseline or stacked: validated for colour-blind readers in light and dark (ΔE 9.1 and 9.0). A legend, a tooltip and Show as a table, as today.

The stills run on a review world: Munchly Foods and four fictional clients with 180 days of batches, about 4 in 10 of them leaving some packs destroyed. Every company and figure is fictional and illustrative.

The options

Option A · Food, by day

Recommended

The first figure stays the money, Recovered, 30 days, with its sparkline. The card under Agents at work stops repeating it and shows the food instead: each day's kilos kept out of landfill (sold or given) rise from the baseline in green, and the kilos destroyed (no channel took them) hang under it, hatched in clay, on one scale. The headline gives both totals and the share saved; the tooltip adds the packs destroyed, what the day recovered and the batches closed.

Trade-offs
  • The two money views become one money and one food view: nothing on the Overview repeats.
  • Each day's misses show as their own bar, so a bad day stands out (20 Sep: 2.4 t destroyed).
  • Money by day moves to the tooltip and the table, no longer drawn.
What the build touches
  • a closed batch keeps the packs it destroyed: sc.batches.destroyed (migration 0012), set by supply.close_batch from the journey's ledger (destroyed at the godown or by the client) and by the synthetic world's hydrate
  • the dashboard's day figures gain savedKg, destroyedKg, destroyed, and the range's totals and the range before (kilos by the SKU's kg_per_unit): platform.js, dashboard.py, the contract, held together by fixtures
  • design3's console.jsx RecoveredChart replaced by the food chart, then the port's RecoveredChart.svelte; the first figure, Agents at work and the rest unchanged

Option B · Recovered and written off

The card keeps the money and adds what was lost: what closed batches recovered as today's line and wash above the baseline, and what the packs they destroyed had cost (written off, at the client's cost) hatched under it, on one scale. The first figure gives up its sparkline, which repeated the card, for the food: a bar of the share saved, and the kilos destroyed.

Trade-offs
  • The card reads in rupees, as the Overview does today, with the loss beside the gain.
  • The first figure changes too: its sparkline goes, and the food takes its place.
  • Written off is small beside recovered (₹9.5 lakh against ₹1.2 crore), so its line hugs the baseline.
What the build touches
  • a closed batch keeps the packs it destroyed: sc.batches.destroyed (migration 0012), set by supply.close_batch from the journey's ledger (destroyed at the godown or by the client) and by the synthetic world's hydrate; the written-off value needs each SKU's cost (skus.cost, set for the synthetic SKUs)
  • the dashboard's day figures gain writtenOff, destroyedKg and the totals
  • design3's chart and first figure, then the port's RecoveredChart.svelte and Kpi

Option C · Food, so far

The first figure stays the money, with its sparkline. The card shows the food as it adds up over the range: the kilos kept out of landfill as a green band from the baseline, and the kilos destroyed stacked on it, hatched, each named where the range ends (154 t kept out, 11.4 t destroyed). The days' own figures are in the tooltip and the table.

Trade-offs
  • Reads as a running record of the food saved: the shape the platform tells clients.
  • Calm: no spikes, and the destroyed band's thickness is the loss so far.
  • A single bad day is harder to see; the by-day figures sit in the tooltip and the table.
