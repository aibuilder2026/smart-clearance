Tally's tax invoice, for a shop

SC-148's layout: the distributor with his GSTIN, PAN, FSSAI licence and bank; the shop as buyer, Unregistered, with its address and pincode; the place of supply, Maharashtra (27); CGST and SGST within the state; the HSN summary; the amounts in words. No IRN: a sale to an unregistered shop is B2C, which the portal does not take.

His own series, from the history on

Each distributor numbers in his own sales series: RT/26-27/ for Rakesh Traders, LA/26-27/ for Lakshmi Agencies. The history's orders come first (115 and 99 invoices, July to September), then the story's: the chips' 31 are RT/26-27/1279 to 1309, then the Mango Drink's 52.

Issued as the shop orders

The distributor bills an order as it is booked, and the van delivers it with the invoice. So each invoice is dated when its shop ordered, and the Paperwork agent lays out its PDF. Munchly's and the distributor's figures stay as they are. The distributor collects the GST and files it as B2C sales in his GSTR-1.

The options

Each is the app itself on its stub at the story's close, with only these screens changed: Ganesh ji (kirana) on his phone, Rakesh Traders (distributor) and Priya on a laptop.

Option A · With each order

Recommended

The invoice sits with the order it bills, and opens on paper in a sheet with its PDF, as every other paper does since SC-130.

  • The kirana: his order's figures follow the invoice: before GST, the GST, what he paid and his margin. “Tax invoice · RT/26-27/1279” opens it. His Orders name each order's invoice.
  • The distributor: the scheme's row names its invoices and the GST he collected. Each shop's order shows its invoice and total, and opens it. The batch's papers gain the sales register.
  • Priya: the pack gains one card, “Tax invoices to kiranas”: the register beside the pack, each number opening its invoice.
Trade-offs
  • Each invoice is one tap from its order, wherever the order shows.
  • The same paper sheet and PDF as the distributor's and the food bank's papers.
  • Rakesh's 31 shops make a long register; it scrolls inside its sheet.

Option B · The bill book

The invoices are kept together, as Tally's books keep them.

  • The kirana: his Orders becomes Orders and bills, a bill book of every invoice he was given, by month. An order's page shows its invoice in full, under the figures.
  • The distributor: his batch page gains an Invoices tab: the count, the taxable value, the GST he collected and the register, each invoice in a sheet. Orders points to it.
  • Priya: the pack gains the register as one card, as in A.
Trade-offs
  • The kirana sees every bill in one place, as he keeps them.
  • The distributor's register is a page of its own, with what he files.
  • A full paper on a phone page is long; a new tab on the batch page.

Option C · As PDFs

The fewest new screens: each invoice is the Paperwork agent's PDF, named where its order is and downloaded from there. Nothing is drawn as a paper in the app.

  • The kirana: his order's figures follow the invoice, with one button for its PDF. His Orders name each invoice.
  • The distributor: each shop's order names its invoice, with its PDF. The batch's papers gain all of them as one PDF.
  • Priya: the pack gains one card listing the invoices, each with its PDF, and all of them as one.
Trade-offs
  • The least to build and the least on screen.
  • Nobody reads an invoice without downloading it; on the stub and the guided demo the PDF is printed from a page that is never shown.
