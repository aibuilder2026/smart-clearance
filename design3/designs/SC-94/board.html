Expiry day

SC-94 · Munchly's workspace · three options for how expiry day reads: the packs no channel took, expired at the godown and settled by the client's expiry policy, on Priya's Execution and in Anita's Paperwork. Every company, person and figure is fictional.

The maintainer (8 Oct):

  • “There can be scenarios when food will be left in go down, as per client setting, it should follow Full Credit or price support depending upon what is chosen.”
  • “Clicking on report now should be treated as Food is being expired and full journey should be realized and completed.”
  • Asked: price support only means the gap is paid and the stock stays with the distributor; Report now closes everything as it stands; the report falls due at best-before.

What is built already

backend-api runs expiry day (Report now, or the report's own timer at best-before): open lines close as they stand, the papers follow, and the report settles the packs left at the godown by the client's expiry policy (money.js expirySettlement): an expiry paper, the destruction certificate counting the packs the client destroys, the GST memo reversing their credit, the ledger and the closing push. What these options decide is how the screens show it. The example is your local chips case: 24 of 588 kirana packs ordered, the lot sold at the ₹14.20 counter, 564 packs left at Kalamna Market godown, net ₹11,282 of ₹21,770.

The paper, in every option

Paperwork gains the expiry paper, set like the price-support credit note: the packs at the dealer price and the credit; under full credit, the client's own costs on destroying them (disposal, EPR, the GST reversal); under no returns, a notice with no credit.

Option A · The card settles

Recommended

The Left at the godown card becomes Expired at the godown on expiry day: the lines that left packs, then the policy as a chip with the credit, one sentence saying who takes the packs and who destroys them, and Open the paper.

Trade-offs
  • One card tells the whole ending: what was left, and how it settled.
  • The figures stay few on Execution; the breakdown is on the paper.
  • The card changes its name on expiry day, which a reader has to notice.
