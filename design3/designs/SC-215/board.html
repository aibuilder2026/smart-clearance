The console: fill a kirana scheme for a demo

SC-215 · smartclearance-console.web.app · three options for a demo control that fills a live client's open kirana scheme as the story has it, each built on the console itself. Munchly's journey stands where a judge leaves the Mango Drink: the plan approved, the Hindi scheme sent to Lakshmi Agencies' 58 kiranas, two of them ordered on the phone. Every company, person and figure is fictional.

The maintainer's request (15 Oct), on SC-212's finding:

  • The finding: a judge can't fill the Mango Drink's scheme alone. Each kirana can order only a few dozen packets, and the scheme goes to 58 shops. In the 13 Oct recording, 50 of them ordered through the API, which a judge can't do. So DEMO.md has the judge order as two shops and close the window, and the unsold packets end up destroyed at the godown.
  • “Can we take this up and update the DEMO.md with proper screenshots, make sure you locally run the playwright automation suite (just for Mango suite) end to end locally for this use case and then raise PR.”

The console now

A live client's Agents tab lists each agent's scheduled runs and timers, each with its button (SC-79): the offer window under Outreach, with Close now. Nothing stands in for the shops: a scheme fills only when the shops order.

What all three share

What the fill does

  • Every shop that hasn't answered orders its share as the story has it, each as its own shop, through the same order path as the phone.
  • So each order gets its own tax invoice from the distributor, in his own series, with its PDF from Paperwork.
  • Each order stays within the shop's cap (4 × its 14-day sales) and the packets left. If packets are still left after the story's shares, the next shops with room take them, until the scheme is full.
  • A shop that said Not this time is left alone.

When it can be used

  • Only on a live demo workspace (one built from the story), and only while a scheme is open.
  • Only for staff who may configure clients: Neha, not Support.
  • Otherwise its button is off and says why: “Once the scheme is sent”, “Every shop has answered”, “The scheme is full”.

What it leaves

  • One audit line in the staff member's name: “Filled MF-2410-118's kirana scheme for Munchly Foods: 50 shops ordered 1,320 packets”.
  • The scheme closes full: 52 of 58 shops, 1,372 packets, on Priya's Execution as each order lands. The van round can then run to every shop.
  • It is marked For demos wherever it shows. It stands in for people, and can't be undone.

Option A · Beside Close now, on Outreach

Recommended

A row of its own under Outreach's stop, below the offer window: Shops yet to answer, marked For demos, with how many have ordered and the packets left, and Fill now. It asks first, as a timer does. Outreach's inspector lists it with the offer window.

Where
The Agents tab: under Outreach's stop and in its inspector, beside the offer window's Close now, while a scheme is open.
Trade-offs
The same place and the same pattern as the journey controls a judge already uses (SC-79's option A), so there is nothing new to learn. The two ways the scheme can end sit side by side: fill it, or close it short. It reads as one more control, so the For demos mark has to say that it acts for the shops.
What the build touches
design3 console.jsx (the trigger's words, its row, its question) and core/platform.js (the schedule's fill row, firing it); the frontend's console journey.ts and the row; the contract's JourneyTrigger (a new key, offer.fill, and the scheme's counts); backend-api controls.py (the schedule and firing) over a new steps.fill_scheme on the order path, with tests.

Option B · One choice when the scheme ends

No new row. The offer window's Close now becomes End now…, and opens a sheet that asks how the scheme ends: Fill it as the story has it (for demos) or Close it short, with what each leaves at the godown.

Where
The offer window's row under Outreach and in its inspector; a sheet in the middle of the screen (from the bottom on a phone).
Trade-offs
One control for how the scheme ends, with the choice made explicit, and the fewest rows. But the fill hides behind a timer's button. Today's one-click close, the sad path's step, gains a choice. And the sheet is a two-way decision where the other timers ask one question.
What the build touches
As A, with the sheet in place of the offer window's question and no extra row; the contract's offer window gains the scheme's counts.

Option C · Stand in for the partners

A card of its own above the agents: Stand in for the partners. It holds what the story's people would do when no one is there to do it, starting with Lakshmi Agencies' kiranas · Fill the scheme, with room for more later (Feeding India's pickup is shown as a later step).

Where
The top of the Agents tab, above the legend and the pipeline, while something is waiting on a partner.
Trade-offs
The most honest framing: these steps act for people, not agents, and they are kept apart from the agents' runs and timers. It has room to grow, such as a pickup or a buyer's yes. But it is a new surface on the Agents tab, and the scheme's two endings are split between the card and Outreach's Close now.
What the build touches
As A, with a new card component in place of the row; the contract lists the stand-ins apart from the triggers.

Open questions

  • Packets still left after the story's shares. If the shops ordered less on the phone than the story has them, the story's shares no longer fill the scheme. The options top up the next shops with room until it is full. Should the fill stop at the story's shares instead, and leave the rest open?
  • Shops that said Not this time. The fill leaves them alone, since they answered. Should it order for them too?
