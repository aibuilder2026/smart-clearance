What is off today

  • The quarter is a sample. Its own note says so: the totals are the walkthrough's, and the weekly split, the channel mix and the BRSR rows are illustrative constants. Its CO₂e never moves, it counts the story's chips batch twice, and nothing on it changes when a ledger posts.
  • A batch's figures read the plan, not what happened. After the leftover run (444 of 588 packets ordered, 144 packs left at the godown) the batch view said ₹1,224 of credit kept and 217.6 kg diverted; the ledger has ₹1,094.40 and 194.56 kg. The ITC memo was stamped "ITC KEPT" with nothing reversed, though ₹129.60 was; the destruction certificate said 0; the money panel's lines did not add up to its net, and it said the kiranas "came in as planned".
  • Two rules miss the packs left at the godown. money.js's realised leaves them out of the ITC reversal and keeps the plan's costs avoided. money.js and backend-api agree with each other; both are fixed in SC-122. Everything else (the plan, the write-off, the invoice, the price support, the expiry settlement, who keeps what) checks out.
  • Finance and ESG are not batch based. Paperwork shows one batch in focus; Finance & ESG opens on the quarter; neither has a way to move between batches.

What all three share

A batch's own page

The operator's batch head from SC-112 (the pack, the name, the id, the distributor, how it ended), with three tabs: Money (what happened against what destroying it would have cost), Papers (the GST on the batch, then the document pack, each with its number) and Impact (the BRSR line and its evidence). Paperwork becomes the Papers tab; Anita opens on Papers, Vikram on Impact.

What happened, not the plan

Every batch figure is its posted ledger: credit kept on the packs sold under tax invoices, reversed on the packs destroyed or given away; kilos diverted, resold or donated, and destroyed; the costs avoided only on what was not destroyed (SC-122).

Live, by period

Q2 FY27, Q3 FY27 or this year so far, summed from the cleared batches' ledgers (SC-124), and read again when a ledger posts. A batch still out is listed as out, never as a figure.

A · One ledger

Recommended

Finance and ESG share one page, the ledger. The period, a reading (Money, GST, Impact), the period's one figure with its working, the period drawn as its batches (each as wide as its figure, the bin hatched), and the batches by month. Every row is a batch; it opens the batch's page.

Open as AnitaOpen as VikramA batch with packs left

Why

  • Batch based by construction: the period is its batches, drawn and listed; nothing is a total without its rows.
  • One ledger, two readings (PRODUCT.md's fourth principle): Anita opens on GST, Vikram on Impact, on the same rows.
  • Leads with one figure and its working, not tiles over a table (DESIGN.md).

Trade-offs

  • The GST return's own lines (Table 4(A)(5), 4(B)(1)) are in the GST reading's working and on each batch, not laid out as a return.
  • No to-do list of its own: an open batch sits at the top as still out.

What the build touches

Report.svelte becomes the ledger and the batch page (Money, Papers, Impact); Paperwork moves into the Papers tab; the Finance and ESG navigation becomes Ledger and Batches. backend-api: a ledger route by period (SC-124).

B · Close the quarter

Each role gets the home its job asks for. Anita's is the quarter's close: its batches through four stops (papers drafted, invoices issued, reviewed, in the GST return), what is left to chase, the return's own figures, and the batches as cards by month. Vikram's is the BRSR builder: Principle 6's rows worked from the batches, each opening on the batches that make it.

Open as AnitaOpen as VikramA batch with packs left

Why

  • Task first: Anita sees at once whether the quarter is closed, and the GSTR-3B lines she files.
  • Vikram's rows are the BRSR form's own, each traced to its batches and their papers.

Trade-offs

  • Two homes to build and keep in step; the money reading (recovered, better than the bin) is only on the cards and the batch page.
  • Cards are bulkier than rows: a long quarter scrolls further.

What the build touches

Report.svelte becomes two screens by role (Close, BRSR) and the batch page; a GST return summary and BRSR rows from backend-api (SC-124).

C · The year as a timeline

Finance and ESG read the same chart: every cleared batch a bar on the day it cleared, in the reading chosen (recovered against what destroying it would have cost, credit kept against reversed, kilos diverted against destroyed), with the months summed under it. A bar opens the batch beside the chart (a sheet on phones), then its own page.

Open as AnitaOpen as VikramA batch with packs left

Why

  • Time and size at a glance: which months cleared most, and which batches were big.
  • The would-be write-off sits behind every bar in the Money reading.

Trade-offs

  • The batch is reached by aiming at a bar; a list of batches by name lives only in Batches.
  • Bars cleared close together stand side by side, so the day is approximate; dense quarters crowd.

What the build touches

Report.svelte becomes the chart, the inspector and the batch page; a ledger route by period with each batch's cleared day (SC-124).
