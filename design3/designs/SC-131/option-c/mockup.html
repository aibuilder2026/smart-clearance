SC-131 design review

Option C · The mark fills

Real progress inside the mark: the squircle starts as its outline, and the brand green rises in it as the first screen's files and reads arrive, by their bytes; the white route shows where the green has reached. Waiting on the platform after the downloads, the mark breathes gently.

What to look at

  • The level: the code's bytes fill it to seven tenths, each of the platform's reads the rest; it never goes back. The build knows the bytes from its own list of the first screen's files.
  • Waiting on the platform (a cold start): the mark breathes, 0.97 to 1.035 every 1.6 s, so it never looks frozen.
  • Ready: the mark fills, its outline goes, the pin lands and pings, and the mark opens into the window.
  • 3G, Console · first load: the cover paints at 2.4 s with the page's first bytes (today: blank until 7.9 s), says it is loading the console, then connecting to the platform, adds "Slow connection · still loading" at 5.4 s, and opens at 11.6 s with no false "did not answer" (today: at 15.4 s, then it opens anyway at 17.7 s).
  • 3G, Landing page: the cover at 2.4 s (today 6.9 s) lifts at 5.0 s, once the first screen can be read on the plate's soft preview; the plate sharpens in place at 12.5 s and the film starts after it (today the loader gives up at 15.2 s onto a page with no plate).
  • No green dots. The tracker under the splash is gone; the words say what it is waiting for.
  • Every timeline is an estimate from the 9 Oct measurements with the shared fixes in; the build measures the real ones.
