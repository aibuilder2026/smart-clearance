Loading on a slow line

SC-131 · console.smartclearance.com and smartclearance.com · three options for how the console's splash and the landing page's loader carry a slow connection, each played over the real pages on a simulated Fast, Slow 4G or 3G line. Every company, person and figure on them is fictional.

The maintainer's words, 9 Oct 2026:

I need you to rethink the login/logout splash and reloading splash for console. For slow networks, it does not load properly. Also for the main landing admin screen, the loader effect is good but in slow network it is taking time.
The animation where it shows green dots, that itself is loading a bit slow and looks odd, that needs to be changed, may think about some motion animations, making the logo throb when loading, think along those lines.

The console now, on a slow line

Measured on the live console on 9 Oct, cold cache, 1280 × 800, on Chrome's 3G (400 kbps, 2 s round trips), dark:

On Slow 4G (1.6 Mbps, 560 ms) it is quicker but the same shape: blank to 2.7 s, then the three green stops filling one by one, then the sign-in at 7.4 s.

Why it fails on a slow line
  • Blank until the app's stylesheet arrives. The splash's script is inlined first in the page, but after the app's render-blocking stylesheet, so nothing paints until that is in: 7.9 s on 3G, 2.7 s on Slow 4G (the page itself is in at 2.2 s).
  • It needs the app's stylesheet anyway: its tokens, the kit's tracker and the wordmark come from it, so it cannot stand on its own before it.
  • A false failure. The 8 s limit counts the download of the console's own code (about 230 KB) as the platform not answering: "Waking the platform up…", then "The console did not answer" at 15.4 s, then the console opens anyway at 17.7 s.
  • The green stops stutter. They count only the platform's three reads, so they sit on "Connecting" through the download, and their progress is redrawn by script every frame, which freezes while the browser reads the app's code.
  • Waiting on things it does not need: three fonts (Bricolage alone is 129 KB) and the sign-in's 181 KB plate download beside the code.

The landing page now, on a slow line

Why it is slow
  • Blank until the stylesheets arrive (6.9 s on 3G), for the same reason as the console.
  • It waits for the hero's 369 KB plate (half its progress), while the table's 240 KB plate (far below the fold), the 6.5 MB film (preload="auto") and 393 KB of fonts (118 KB of Devanagari for the loader's one Hindi line) download beside it: 11.7 s on Slow 4G.
  • On 3G it gives up at its 8 s cap, 15.2 s after the click, onto a page with no plate, its heading in a fallback face until about 29 s.

What every option fixes

Paints with the first bytes

  • The cover's styles, inks and wordmark come inline with its script; nothing waits for the app's stylesheet, which no longer holds back the first paint while the cover is up.
  • 3G: the console's cover at about 2.4 s (today 7.9 s), the landing page's at about 2.4 s (today 6.9 s).
  • No green dots. One title and one line say what it is waiting for: loading the console, connecting to the platform, opening.

Never cries wolf

  • A download still arriving is never a failure. The cover says the console did not answer only when a read is refused, the line is offline, or a read has had no answer for 20 s, and then it names it, with Try again.
  • Past 3 s, one quiet line: "Slow connection · still loading". "Waking the platform up…" only when the platform itself is the wait.
  • The connection to the API opens with the page's first bytes, and the console's code for after a sign-in is fetched while the person types.

The first screen first

  • Landing: the hero's poster first and at high priority; the table's plate when it nears the window; the film once the cover has lifted; only the first screen's fonts, and the Hindi line from a few-KB subset.
  • It lifts on time: once the first screen can be read on the plate's soft preview (about 2 KB, inline), at least 1.25 s as now; the plate sharpens in place. 3G: about 5 s (today 15.2 s); Slow 4G: about 2.9 s (today 11.7 s).
  • Fallback faces sized to the brand's, so a late font swaps in place without moving the words.

Each option keeps the exit the maintainer liked: the pin pings once in amber and the mark's squircle opens into a window onto the page (SC-35, SC-51). Screen readers hear each wait once; the page behind is inert and aria-busy; only the loader moves, and only while something loads (WCAG 2.2.2). The timings here are estimates from the 9 Oct measurements with these fixes in; the build measures the real ones on throttled lines.

Option A · The heartbeat

Recommended

The mark is the loader. Drawn whole from the first paint, it beats while anything is still on its way: two pulses a beat (strong, then soft) every 1.3 s, and a ring of its own outline ripples out on the strong pulse. Everything in, the beat settles on a spring, the pin pings once, and the mark opens into the window onto the page.

The console's first load on 3G

The landing page on 3G

Why it works
  • Throbs, as asked. The design system's own breathing (aura-breathe) made a heartbeat, on the mark itself.
  • Never stutters. Transform and opacity only, in CSS: the compositor runs it, so it keeps time while the browser is busy reading the app's code, exactly when today's script-drawn progress freezes.
  • Honest without numbers. It says it is alive and what it is waiting for, in words; it does not claim how far along it is.
  • The same beat everywhere: the first load, signing in and out, the landing page, and the workspace app's live splash, which shares the console's script.
Trade-offs
  • It shows no amount of progress; on a very slow line the words carry it.
Why recommended
The calmest and the cheapest to run; the one that answers the request most directly; works the same on every wait and every phone.

Option B · The route runs

The mark's S is the loader. The route stands faint, and a bright stretch of it runs from the godown dot to the pin, a lap every 1.15 s; as each lap arrives the amber pin throbs. A delivery in transit until everything is in: then the last lap draws the whole route and stays, the pin pings once, and the mark opens into the window.

Real progress inside the mark. Recommended: A, the heartbeat. It is what the request reaches for, the logo throbbing while it loads; it is the only one of the three that the browser keeps smooth while it is busy with the app's code, the exact moment the green dots stutter today; and with the shared fixes it puts the cover on screen at the page's first bytes and never claims a failure that is only a slow download.

What the build would touch

  • design3: console/splash.js and its @splash block in console.css (the tracker goes; the cover's styles come inline), site/loader.js and its block in site.css (the mark's motion, lifting on the first screen), and the prototypes' copy of the words.
  • The console (frontend/console): hooks.server.ts inlines the whole cover and keeps the app's stylesheet from blocking the first paint, opens the API's connection early; the error state only on a refused or silent read; the code for after a sign-in fetched while the person types.
  • The landing page (frontend/admin): the poster first, the table's plate when near, the film after the lift, the plate's soft preview inline, font subsets and sized fallbacks.
  • The workspace app's live splash shares the console's script (SC-73), so it changes with it.
  • DESIGN.md: the loader's rules (SC-35) and the console's splash.
