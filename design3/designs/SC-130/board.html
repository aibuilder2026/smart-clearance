Partner portals

SC-130 · Munchly's workspace · three ways for the distributor, the kirana and the food bank to see their own history and their papers. Every company, person and figure is fictional; every figure is money.js's.

The maintainer (9 Oct):

“Distributors UI needs some change, they should be able to see Batch level information of what happened for past and present events, based on the stocks they that are at risk or were at risk and got cleared, They should see GST invoices, creditnote and other finance documents on their portal too.”

“Kiranas should be able to see offers and past order, where they were accepted or declined/expired on their portal.”

“Food bank partners also should be able to see past pickups, and donation receipts /certificates.”

What they see today

  • Rakesh bhai sees only the batch in a journey now. Today carries its plan, invoice draft and how he ends whole; Orders lists this scheme's orders. Nothing tells him what happened to the six batches he has already cleared, and none of their papers reach him.
  • A kirana sees the open offer, or "No offers today", and one order. Nothing records the schemes it let expire, and there is no way to decline one.
  • Meera sees the pickup in front of her. Her two earlier pickups for Munchly, and their receipts, are nowhere.

What all three share

Each sees only its own

A distributor sees the batches at his godown, the papers he issues (his tax invoice and its e-way bill check) and those issued to him (Munchly's price-support and expiry credit notes), plus copies of what concerns his packs (the food bank's receipt, the destruction certificate). Munchly's GST ITC memo and FSSAI checklist stay Munchly's. A kirana sees its own offers and orders; a food bank its own pickups and receipts.

Declined, and expired with its reason

A new Not this time under the open offer declines it (the offer stays open for the 48 hours if the shop changes its mind). An offer the shop did not answer expired, and says why: the scheme filled first, or its 48 hours ended. The history's shops that did not order count as expired.

Every paper on paper

Each paper opens as the operator sees it (the same paper components), with Download PDF: the Paperwork agent's own PDF on the live workspace, the paper printed in the prototype.

A · Batch by batch

Recommended

Every partner reads its own batches, offers and pickups the way Priya reads hers (SC-112, SC-121): a list of what was and is at stake, each opening its own page. Rakesh gets Batches (in a journey now, everything he cleared by month under one figure, the stock the Watcher reads), and each batch's page has What happened, Money (how he ended whole) and Papers. A kirana's Offers lists every earlier offer under the open one, each opening on what was offered and what came of it; Orders says what each order earned. A food bank's Pickups lists the coming then the collected, each opening on its tracker, its receipt and its FSSAI checklist.

Why

  • Batch level, as asked: each batch is one place with what happened, the money and the papers, past or present.
  • One way of reading for everyone: the same list-to-page pattern Priya has (SC-112, SC-121), so the build reuses the batch head, the tabs and the paper sheet.
  • Phone first: a list, then a page; the distributor's four tabs stay four (Label photo stays on the desktop sidebar and is opened from its request).

Trade-offs

  • Finding one paper means knowing its batch; there is no register of every credit note.
  • The kirana's offers and orders are two lists over the same offers.

What the build touches

design3 trade.jsx first (DistBatches and the batch page, Offers with Not this time, the offer page, Orders, Pickups and the pickup page), then core's trade screens and the live projection. backend-api: partner-scoped reads (a distributor's batches with their cases and papers, a shop's offers with their outcome, a food bank's pickups with their receipts) and the decline step.

B · One timeline

Each partner gets one History: everything that happened to it, newest first, by month, down one rail. Each batch, offer or pickup is a card with its outcome, one line of what came of it and its papers as chips; What happened opens it in place. A filter narrows the rail by outcome. The kirana's orders live in its History (Offers keeps the open offer with Not this time); the food bank's receipts and checklists are chips on each pickup.

Why

  • The story in order: past and present on one rail, each batch's moments a tap away.
  • One new screen per role, the same for all three, with the papers as chips beside what they record.

Trade-offs

  • Long: a busy distributor scrolls through months of cards; a batch's money is one line, not a statement.
  • Cards opened in place make a long page longer; there is no page to link a batch to.

What the build touches

design3 trade.jsx: a History screen per role (the rail, the cards, in-place disclosure, the filter), Offers' Not this time; then core and the live projection. backend-api: the same partner-scoped reads as A, with each batch's moments, and the decline step.

C · Statement and papers

Each partner gets a register, led by the one figure that matters to it, a row a paper (or a scheme, or a receipt), with the batch on every row and an export. Rakesh's Account is a statement with Munchly: what Munchly credited him since July, every paper by date with its amount, or by batch with how each ended. A kirana's Schemes lists every scheme with its share, status, what it paid and its margin. A food bank's Receipts lists every receipt with packs, kilos and meals, for its own annual report.

Why

  • Finance first: every GST invoice and credit note in one place, with the total Munchly credited, ready to reconcile in Tally and to export.
  • The food bank's register is what it files: receipts, packs, kilos, meals.

Trade-offs

  • What happened to a batch is a footnote (how it ended), not its story; the present batch lives only on Today.
  • Tables are dense on a phone; the columns fold to two.

What the build touches

design3 trade.jsx: Account, Schemes and Receipts registers with their exports, Offers' Not this time; then core and the live projection. backend-api: the partner-scoped reads, a statement of a distributor's papers by date, and the decline step.
