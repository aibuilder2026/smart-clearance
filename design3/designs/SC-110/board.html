Donation receipt

SC-110 · Munchly's workspace · three options for the receipt a food bank issues when it collects a donation: a real paper, per food bank, that Meera can open and Munchly keeps as BRSR evidence. Every company, person and figure is fictional; the food banks are the story's partners, and their receipts and numbers here are invented.

The maintainer (9 Oct):

  • “I dont see any receipts in the app for donations....”
  • On how many kinds: per food bank, as the Journey Map has it: Feeding India issues an in-app receipt; India FoodBanking Network a donation acknowledgement (CSR / 80G, indicative).

Where it stands

Meera's collected pickup says In-app receipt issued · 86 drinks served · shared with Munchly for its BRSR table, and backend-api's audit log says it issued the receipt, but no receipt exists: not in Meera's Pickups, not among the Mango Drink's six papers, not in Vikram's evidence. The prototype has the same sentence and no receipt either.

The paper, per food bank

The same facts in each food bank's own paper: who gave what to whom, the batch and its date, the packs and their weight, when it was collected and by whom, where it was served, and the meals (a pack a meal, indicative). Feeding India's is a Donation receipt, stamped RECEIVED. India FoodBanking Network's is a Donation acknowledgement, stamped ACKNOWLEDGED, with what a CSR file needs: the value at Munchly's cost (money.js, indicative) and the CSR activity, and a plain line that it is not a tax certificate (section 80G covers gifts of money). India FoodBanking Network needs 21+ days and 100+ units, so its paper is shown with the Mango Drink's figures for comparison only. Options A and C use these papers as they are; B sets the receipt under the FSSAI checklist.

Option A · A paper in the pack

Recommended

The receipt is issued when the food bank marks the packs collected, as the seventh paper of the batch's document pack, issued by the food bank (Feeding India's Donation receipt, India FoodBanking Network's Donation acknowledgement), with its number and its PDF. Meera opens it from her collected pickup (View, a sheet on a phone). Anita finds it in the pack beside the FSSAI checklist; Vikram in Evidence, and the batch's BRSR evidence names its number.

Trade-offs
  • One paper system: the same card, the same paper, the same PDF and export as every other paper.
  • The receipt is where an auditor looks for the batch's evidence.
  • The pack grows to seven papers for a donated batch.
  • The donor's checklist and the food bank's receipt stay two papers, each from its own issuer.
What the build touches
backend-api: the receipt as a paper at collection (steps.collect), numbered in each food bank's own series, in the case's papers; the agents: its PDF template; the contract: the paper's fields (food bank, packs, kilos, meals, where served); design3 trade.jsx Pickups, finance.jsx Paper and the stub's papers, then core Pickups.svelte, Paper.svelte, DocCard and the evidence line (SC-107).

Option B · One donation paper

The donor's FSSAI checklist and the food bank's receipt become one paper, the Surplus-food handover: the checklist on top, and the food bank's receipt (or acknowledgement) stamped beneath it when the packs are collected. It takes the FSSAI checklist's place in the pack and beside Meera's pickup, where the checklist already sits.

Trade-offs
  • One paper tells the donation from the donor's ticks to the food bank's receipt.
  • The pack keeps its six papers.
  • Two issuers on one page: the donor's checklist and the food bank's receipt, which an auditor may want apart.
  • The FSSAI checklist, a named paper today, changes its name.
What the build touches
As A, with the FSSAI paper reworked into the handover instead of a new paper, and its PDF template reworked.

Option C · A receipts register

Receipts live in a register of their own, not in a batch's pack: Meera keeps Your receipts under her pickups, and Vikram's Finance & ESG gains Donation receipts under BRSR Core, every receipt of the quarter with its PDF. The batch's pack and its evidence line link to its receipt.

Trade-offs
  • Built for the auditor: the quarter's donated kilos and every receipt behind them, in one list.
  • Meera sees all her receipts, across brands, in one place.
  • A new list in two screens, and a quarter-wide read from backend-api.
  • Anita finds the batch's receipt one step away from its pack.
What the build touches
As A for the paper, plus a register: a quarter's receipts from backend-api (a new read), Pickups and Report sections in design3 and core.
