Questions we hear first

Is near-expiry stock the same as expired stock?

No. Near-expiry stock is still within its best-before date, often with weeks left, but too short-dated for the quick-commerce warehouses, which take stock only with enough of its shelf life left. Smart-Clearance acts while there is still time to sell it.

Whose stock is it, and who sells it?

Usually the distributor's, who bought it. The agents list and invoice in the distributor's name only after a one-time permission, which the distributor can pause at any time.

Does anything happen without a person?

Nothing is listed, messaged or shipped before a person approves the plan, with the money on screen. The agents prepare everything up to that tap, and do the running around after it.

What happens to the GST credit?

Stock sold on tax invoices keeps its input credit; destroying or donating it reverses the credit. The agents draft the invoice, the credit note and the GST memo for finance.

How does a manufacturer start?

With a pilot on one distributor's stock for 90 days, in a workspace at your own address, set up for how your stock really moves.
