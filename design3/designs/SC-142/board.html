The options

Option A · The Record tab

Recommended

Batches lists the batches in view as today, then every batch that has cleared, by month, each with its photos counted and what it recovered. A batch's page gains a fourth tab, Record: the photos sent for it, together, with what Vision read; the yeses in a card of their own; and the audit trail, every step day by day by the agent or the person who took it, filtered to people or agents, with a CSV. Rakesh's batch page gains a Photos tab: the photos he sent, and Priya's yes on the destruction.

Trade-offs
  • The record has one home, on every batch, in a journey or long cleared.
  • The photos lead, so what was sent is seen first; the trail answers who did what and when.
  • One tab more on two pages; Batches grows a long list once many batches clear (months group it).
What the build touches
design3 first (screens/brand.jsx Batches, finance.jsx the batch page, trade.jsx), then core's screens; backend-api: each batch's photos with signed links (the label photo, the destruction's two) and its record (the case's steps and its audit lines) for the operator, and his own photos in the distributor's partner view; the contract; the history's label photos stored with its cases.

Option B · Evidence on the timeline

Batches is one table, In view or Cleared; a cleared row shows its photos as thumbnails. A batch's page opens on its Timeline: every step day by day, the label photo where it was sent with what Vision read, the destruction's two photos where they were sent with Vision's checks, each yes in amber. Audit lines shows each person's step as the audit log keeps it (when, who, what, which batch). Rakesh's What happened carries his photos at the moments he sent them.

Trade-offs
  • Tells the batch as a story: each photo next to what it led to.
  • The audit log and the story are one thing, with its raw lines a switch away.
  • A long page; to see just the photos, Priya scrolls.

Option C · The batch file

Batches switches between In view and Cleared; cleared batches are files, the label photo on each cover with its photo count. A batch's page leads with its evidence: every photo and every yes across the top, then Money, Papers, Impact and an Audit log tab, a table of when, who, as what, and what, filtered and exported as CSV. Rakesh's Label photo becomes Photos: every photo he sent Munchly, batch by batch, with what was made of each.

Trade-offs
  • The strongest for an audit: the evidence and the log, nothing in between.
  • Rakesh finds every photo in one place.
  • Changes the most: Batches becomes a gallery when cleared, the page's head grows, the distributor's nav changes.

Recommended: option A

The Record tab keeps the record apart from the money and the papers, where an operator or an auditor goes looking for it, and it reads the same on a batch in a journey and one long cleared. The photos lead, so “what picture was sent” is answered at a glance; the trail below answers “what happened, and who said yes”. Rakesh gets the same idea in his own words, a Photos tab on each batch. B tells the story better but buries the photos in a long page; C is the strongest for an auditor but changes the most and makes Batches a gallery.
