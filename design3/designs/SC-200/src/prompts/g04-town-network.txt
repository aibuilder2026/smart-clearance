A tilt-shift photograph of a handcrafted miniature diorama of an Indian town at dawn, seen from above at a gentle angle: a small factory with a chimney, a warehouse godown with two tiny trucks, a lane of tiny kirana shops with colourful cloth awnings, a small white van, and a highway curving into the distance. Thin glowing emerald-green threads of light run along the roads and connect every building like a living network, with small bright points of light along them. Soft morning mist and warm sunrise light, magical and highly detailed.
