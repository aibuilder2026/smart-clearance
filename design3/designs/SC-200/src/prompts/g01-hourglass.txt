Inside a dim, vast Indian warehouse godown at dusk, a colossal glass hourglass taller than a person stands on the concrete floor between tall steel racks stacked with plain brown cardboard cartons. Instead of sand, a slow stream of small bright red snack packets falls through its narrow waist and piles up in the lower bulb, while the upper bulb is almost empty. Shafts of warm golden light pour through high windows and catch floating dust.
