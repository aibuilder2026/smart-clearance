The hero as it is

Desktops draw the town full-bleed, its hazy sky running up behind the heading into the page. Phones and tablets put the heading on the plain page, and the town in a card under it.

What both options share

  • One picture on every screen. Phones and tablets lose the card: the hero is full-bleed, the nav glass over it, and the town's sky runs up into the page as it does on desktops.
  • The sky is the plates' own. By day the plate's warm haze (#f3efeb), by night its navy (#000c26), deepening from the page's ground. Nothing is painted over the town and nothing moves.
  • The copy centred on phones too, on the on-plate inks, over the sky.
  • The caption docked on the town's foot in glass, with the zoom controls under it, all within the first screen at 390 × 844 and 820 × 1180.
  • Round 1 unchanged: a card at once on pointing, the zoom about the node after 0.6 s, a click to keep the card, the camera back on looking away; the tour, its pace, Pause and Play.

Two options

A · The horizon

Recommended

The town sits lower, under an open sky. The heading, its line and the buttons stand on clear sky; the plate's top fades into it, so the town rises from a hazy horizon just below the buttons. On phones and tablets the same picture stands upright: sky above, the town filling the rest of the screen, the caption on its foot.

  • Why: the copy always has a calm ground, by day and by night, with no blur needed. Every place and every agent stays in view at rest, and a phone shows as much of the town across as the card did (about 56% of its width; B shows 37%).
  • Trade-offs: on desktops the town gives up about a tenth of the frame's height to the sky, so the caption sits over the kiranas' foreground rather than the road.
  • The build would touch: site/site.css (the hero's sky and frame at every width, the caption docked) and its port in frontend/admin; town.jsx and Town.svelte only for the haze band, which now sits at the stage's top edge.

Open option A

B · The haze

The town stays big and behind the copy, as the desktop is today, and the depth renderer's tilt-shift haze, which round 1 used only once the camera neared, now holds at rest: the top of the town goes soft and pale behind the heading, and comes into focus from the buttons down. On phones and tablets the town rises from behind the buttons.

  • Why: the town is larger in every frame, and on desktops the picture barely changes from what was approved; only the haze is new.
  • Trade-offs: what stands in the haze steps out of view at rest: the Landfill and the Lister on desktops, until the camera moves. The ground behind the copy is a blur of the town rather than open sky. On phones the town is cropped closer (about 37% of its width shows).
  • The build would touch: town.jsx and Town.svelte (the haze at rest on every screen, its band measured from the buttons), site.css and its port (the full-bleed frame on phones and tablets).

Open option B

Recommended: A, the horizon. It answers both messages at once: the text gets a clean ground, and phones and tablets get the same sky-merged picture as desktops, with the whole town and every agent in view. B keeps the town bigger, at the cost of a blurred ground and two agents hidden at rest.

Open question

  • The sky is plain, the plates' own colour. Should it be painted instead, with thin clouds by day and still stars at night? That would add two images (about 250 kB) and more texture behind the heading.
