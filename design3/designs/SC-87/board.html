The staff sale, and what is left at the godown

SC-87 · Munchly's workspace · three options for the distributor's staff sale on the Mango Drink batch (MF-2410-118, Lakshmi Agencies, Hyderabad), and for how the packs no channel took read as left at the godown. Every company, person and figure is fictional.

  • “I also see staff sale and Kirana split for Mango drink, something does not add on for this journey.”

Now

The Router's plan for the Mango Drink is 1,372 packs to Lakshmi Agencies' kiranas, 150 to her staff sale at ₹8 and 58 to a food bank (net ₹16,917). SC-86 made backend-api run every line: the staff sale opens when the plan is approved and waits for Lakshmi Agencies to record what sold (POST …/cases/MF-2410-118/staff-sale {sold}), and the figures count only what each channel took. The screens have nothing for it yet: Lakshmi Agencies' Today says “Nothing to do today”, and Execution has no staff sale and no figure for what is left.

What all three share

The sale

  • Lakshmi Agencies runs it at Begum Bazaar godown: 150 packs at ₹8, money.js's 40% of MRP. Staff pay her by UPI; no agent acts.
  • Only the batch's own distributor records it, once: 0 to 150 packs (SC-86's guard).
  • Whatever is not sold stays at the godown.

The figures

  • money.js's realised: each line on what its channel took. The example: 1,180 of 1,372 ordered, 120 of 150 sold to staff, 58 collected: net ₹14,469 of ₹16,917.
  • Left at the godown is the rest, 222 packs: recovered nothing, and still facing the write-off at best-before.

The close

  • Once the offer, the staff sale and the pickup are done, the batch is settled (SC-86), and Priya gets one push: what was recovered of the plan, by channel, and what the godown still holds.
  • The report and Finance read the same figures.

The mockups show the cards on the real kit, beside each other, without the app's sidebar. The build places them on Lakshmi Agencies' Today and on Priya's Execution.

Option A · A card on Today

Recommended

Lakshmi Agencies' Today gains a Staff sale card under the plan: the packs, the price, her UPI code to show staff, and one stepper to record what sold when the sale is over. Priya's Execution gets a matching card, and a Left at the godown card, line by line, once the lines are done.

Trade-offs
  • One record, one tap: the step SC-86 built, as it stands. The quickest to play in a demo.
  • Execution says plainly what each line left behind, with the net beside the plan's.
  • Priya sees nothing until the sale is recorded (2 to 3 days at money.js's pace).
