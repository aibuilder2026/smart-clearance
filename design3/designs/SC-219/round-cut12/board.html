Option A · live action with motion graphics
Recommended

The data hall

A real camera moves through a hyperscale data centre at night, and the stack is laid onto its racks as the voice names each part, the way a keynote shows its infrastructure. Every graphic is tracked to its rack and rides the camera's move.

How it tells the scene

  • Under the hoodThe cooling plant, its pipes in Google's four colours; the title rises over it, then a flash-push into the cold aisle.
  • Four apps, FirebaseThe four apps float into the aisle as glass, an orange scan running down each.
  • One API, Cloud RunA call runs along the cable tray to a rack, which lights blue as brackets snap onto it.
  • The record, Cloud SQLA rack across the aisle lights, and the record writes down its face row by row.
  • Pub/Sub, SchedulerPulses race along the overhead fibre trays; a clock on the timing rack.
  • Nine AI agentsTen racks light one by one with their names: nine on Gemini, the Watcher in plain code. BigQuery and Cloud Storage in the storage hall.
  • GeminiThe liquid-cooled TPU hall, Pro in violet, Flash in blue.
  • Never the moneyThe API's rack in a locked cage, the reserve inside; Priya's yes, and every rack down the hall turns green.

Motion and effects

One slow real camera move a shot, graphics tracked onto it, flash-pushes between shots, light sweeps, cut 10's notes.

Sound

The hall's air, fans rising as racks light, relay clicks, a chime for each part, fibre zips.

To build

About eight more plates and camera moves (Qwen-Image and LTX, about 1.7 hours on this Mac), each reviewed, then the tracking and the composition.

Watch for

Generated footage can bend a rack or invent a sign, so each clip is checked and some are made again. A tracked graphic can slip a few pixels on a fast move.

Option B · motion graphics
Recommended

The blueprint

The data centre drawn as one isometric floor plan in Smart-Clearance's greens, built and lit in motion, the way Google Cloud's own explainers draw a system. Every frame is drawn, so it shows how the parts connect, not only what they are called.

How it tells the scene

  • Under the hoodThe camera starts on one rack's light and pulls back as the hall rises row by row; a band of light crosses the floor under the title.
  • Four apps, FirebaseThe four apps' screens rise at the hall's edge on Firebase's platform.
  • One API, Cloud RunA call runs along the floor to the core, and Cloud Run's containers stack on its racks.
  • The record, Cloud SQLCloud SQL's disks stack up and the records fly in.
  • Pub/Sub, SchedulerEvents run the spine across the floor; the clock tower ticks.
  • Nine AI agentsA row of ten racks, each named with its model, the Watcher grey in plain code; BigQuery's row and Cloud Storage's bucket.
  • GeminiThe accelerator row lights violet and its beams reach each agent, Pro in violet, Flash in blue.
  • Never the moneyA glass wall rises around the API and Gemini's beam stops at it; Priya's yes at the desk, a ring across the floor, everything lit.

Motion and effects

One continuous camera over one plan: draw-ons, extrusions on springs, light sweeps, bloom, packets with trails.

Sound

Soft plucks as each part rises, a low hum, whooshes on the glides, a swell on the yes.

To build

The plan drawn in full and the camera laid on the voice, all in Remotion. No generated footage, so any change is quick.

Watch for

It is a drawing, not footage. The rest of the film is live action, so this scene reads as a diagram, as cut 11's did, but in the brand's own look.

Option C · macro product film
Recommended

Inside the machine

A product film shot in macro inside the machines: fibres, server sleds, a TPU under its cold plate, a locked cage. Each part of the stack is set large as the voice names it, with light running through the hardware.

How it tells the scene

  • Under the hoodLight runs down the fibres; “All on Google Cloud.” set large, Google lighting as it is said.
  • Four apps, FirebaseA rack focus onto the server sleds; “Four apps.” with the apps as glass tiles.
  • One API, Cloud RunThe pulled-out sled lights blue along its edge.
  • The record, Cloud SQLWrites run along every sled, top to bottom.
  • Pub/Sub, SchedulerPulses through a fibre bundle; a timing card's crystal.
  • Nine AI agentsTen sleds light in turn, the Watcher's in plain white.
  • GeminiThe TPU's coolant glows violet; the two models set large with their agents.
  • Never the moneyA locked mesh with one dark appliance; Priya's yes turns its green light on.

Motion and effects

Slow macro moves and rack focus, large type rising out of a mask with a light passing across it, light pulses on the fibres.

Sound

Close and tactile: a fibre's whine, a sled's click, coolant, a lock, a pure tone for the yes.

To build

About seven more macro plates and moves (about 1.5 hours), and large type for every beat.

Watch for

The most cinematic of the three, but it names the parts more than it shows how they connect. Macro footage can melt fine detail.
