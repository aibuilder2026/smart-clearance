The cooling plant of a hyperscale data centre at night: a cathedral-like hall where dozens of huge insulated water pipes run in long parallel lines and sweeping bends, the pipes painted in bold solid glossy colours, blue, green, yellow and red, with valves and flanges, soft overhead light pooling on the polished grey floor, seen from a low, wide, symmetrical viewpoint down the length of the hall, a sense of scale and order.
