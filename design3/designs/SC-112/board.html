Batch first

SC-112 · Munchly's workspace · three ways to navigate the Command Center, the Route Room and Execution from the top down, starting with the batch. Every screen's own design stays as it is: only the way between them changes. Every company, person and figure is fictional.

The maintainer (9 Oct):

“I need you to think a bit on the design and navigation of command center, route room and execution. 1. The design of the individual pages are perfect, no change is needed. 2. The navigability seems a bit odd, everything starts from monitoring of a batch which can be in different states.”

“I need a top down approach starting with batch as the 1st point, think hard and come up with aesthetically pleasing approach.”

Why it feels odd today

  • Two kinds of place in one list. The sidebar puts Route Room and Execution beside the Command Center and Batches, as if they were places of their own. They are screens of one batch.
  • The batch is hidden. Which batch Route Room or Execution opens is a "batch in focus" you cannot see: whichever was opened last (SC-91 had to keep it). On a phone, Route and Live in the tab bar work the same way.
  • The state picks the screen. The Command Center's one button becomes Open Route Room, Review and approve, or Watch execution, depending on the batch's state, so the same card leads to different places.
  • Switching batch half works. The batch tabs (SC-68) sit on the Command Center and the Route Room, but not on Execution, and Paperwork is not in the operator's menu at all.
  • Back is fixed by stage. Execution always goes back to the Route Room, and the Route Room to the Command Center, wherever the person came from.

The shape all three share

The workspace holds the batches; a batch holds its screens. Route Room and Execution stop being places in the sidebar and become two of a batch's screens, next to its journey and its papers.

The same in A, B and C

  • Route Room and Execution leave the sidebar; on phones the tab bar becomes Today, Batches, Reports.
  • Every screen of a batch names its batch in its address, so nothing is hidden.
  • Back goes to where the batch was opened from (Command Center, Batches, Inbox), not to a fixed screen.

Where a batch opens

  • On the screen for where it stands: before Verify its journey; Verify to Approve the Route Room; Execute its Execution; Settle its papers; cleared, its journey again.
  • The Command Center's cards and rows, the sidebar and the Batches screen all open the batch the same way.

What does not change

  • The Command Center, Batches, Setup, Finance & ESG, and every screen's body: the Route Room's card, label, channels and split, Execution's cards, Paperwork.
  • A batch not in a journey still opens its sheet (see the open questions).
  • New in A and B: a Journey view, the Command Center's tracker card, cluster and agents for one batch.

The options

Each option is the workspace app itself, live, signed in as Priya, with the Masala Chips at Approve and the Mango Drink at Verify. Click anything; the pill at the bottom left moves the chips to Execute, Settle or Cleared.

Option A · The batch page

Recommended

A batch is a page of its own, the way an order is. One head for every screen about it (its product, name, id, distributor and where it stands), and its screens as tabs under the head: Journey, Route Room, Execution, Paperwork. The tab for where the batch stands carries a dot, amber while it waits for your yes. The batches in a journey sit in the sidebar by name and stop.

How you move
  • Desktop: Command Center, Batches and Setup, then In a journey: each batch with its product and stop. A batch opens its page; its tabs move between its screens.
  • Phone: the head, then the tabs as a pill row (Journey, Route, Execution, Papers); the tab bar is Today, Batches, Reports.
Trade-offs
  • The plainest top-down shape: workspace, batch, screen, each a level you can see.
  • Tabs are a pattern everyone knows, and every screen keeps its full width.
  • The Route Room shows the product twice (the head, then its own first card).
  • On a phone, switching batch means going back to Today or Batches.
Motion
The tab's thumb slides (the kit's tabs spring, 500/40); the screen under the head rises 6 px in 220 ms; the head stays still. Under reduced motion it lands at once.
What the build touches
A batch frame in core's RoleApp (the head and the tabs), routes /batch/<ref>/<screen>, the sidebar's batches, the Journey view from the Command Center's pieces; the screens' bodies unchanged. The smallest build of the three.

Option B · The batch rail

The batches are a place of their own. Opening Batches, or any batch, turns the sidebar into the batch rail: every batch grouped by where it stands (Needs your yes, In a journey, Watching), and the open batch unfolds its screens under it. The sidebar keeps its width, so every screen keeps its own. On a phone the same tree is a stack: Batches, then a batch with its screens as rows, then the screen.

How you move
  • Desktop: the workspace's menu (Command Center, Batches with an amber count, Setup, Finance & ESG); Batches or any batch drills into the rail; ‹ Command Center at its top drills out.
  • Phone: Batches lists every batch by group; a batch shows its screens as rows with their state, over its tracker card.
Trade-offs
  • The fastest switching: every batch, and how close each is to its date, in view while you work one.
  • The page keeps its own title (Route Room), with the batch in the line under it.
  • The sidebar has two modes, which takes a moment to learn.
  • On a phone a screen is three steps from Today.
Motion
The sidebar drills: the rail enters from the right and the menu leaves left (28 px, 240 ms), and back the other way; the open batch unfolds its screens on the sheet spring (420/40/0.9). Under reduced motion it lands at once.
What the build touches
A second sidebar mode in the kit's Shell (the rail), its groups from the workspace's batches, routes as A, a phone Batches list and batch page, the Journey view; the screens' bodies unchanged.

Option C · The stops lead

A batch is headed by its own tracker, the card the Route Room opens with, and the tracker's stops are the way through it: Detect opens its journey; Verify, Value, Decide and Approve open the Route Room at that part; Execute its Execution; Settle its papers; Report its line in the ledger. The fill says where the batch is; the pill says where you are. Once the card scrolls away the stops stay under the bar. The batch's name switches batch.

How you move
  • Desktop: the nine stops, each a button (Connect is the workspace's, set once in Setup); scrolling the Route Room moves the pill from Verify to Approve.
  • Phone: the stops as chips under the card, and kept under the bar once it scrolls away.
Trade-offs
  • The most our own: the tracker, the product's signature, becomes how you read a batch, top to bottom.
  • Execution and Paperwork gain the Route Room's card, so every screen of a batch shows its days and gates.
  • The tracker shows two things at once (where the batch is, where you are), which needs a careful eye.
  • Four stops share the Route Room, so some stops scroll and others change screen.
Motion
The pill slides along the stops (500/40); going on down the journey the screen rises from below (26 px, 280 ms), going back it drops from above; the kept strip fades in under the bar. Under reduced motion it lands at once.
What the build touches
A batch frame with the Route Room's first card moved into it (the Route Room loses its own copy), the tracker's stops as buttons, the scroll-linked pill, the strip under the bar, routes per stop, the name's menu; the other screens' bodies unchanged. The largest build of the three.

Recommended: A, the batch page. It gives the plainest top-down shape, workspace then batch then screen, with nothing new to learn: a batch gets a head and an address, its screens are tabs, and the batches in a journey are the first thing in the sidebar after the workspace's own places. Every screen keeps its design and its width. It also carries over: Finance's Paperwork and Sustainability's report can become tabs of the same batch page. B is the better switcher when many batches run at once, at the price of a sidebar with two modes. C is the most our own, but asks the tracker to show where the batch is and where you are at the same time, and its stops crowd a phone.
