Smart-Clearance platform design

The design for SC-25: the product's own landing page at smartclearance.com, and the console where Smart-Clearance staff set up each client's workspace. Sixteen screens in design system v3, seeded with Munchly Foods. Nothing is built yet; this is what the build will follow once you say go.

Landing page Miniature India · comp-led Console Agent pipeline · code-led Design only 4 Oct 2026 SC-25 in Jira

How the pages connect

Two new pages join the guided demo and Munchly's workspace, which already exist. The landing page sends each visitor to one of four places; the console's sidebar holds everything a super admin does.

smartclearance.com

One near-expiry carton grown to the size of a godown, parked in a miniature Indian town, and the page follows where its packs go. It belongs to no single client; Munchly Foods appears as the customer story. Every figure comes from the Journey Map v4.1 through the app's money model.

L5 Your own workspace

The software-as-a-service model: one island per manufacturer, each at its own address, all tied to one product. Then what each team gets, and what it connects to.

In the build
The islands carry the v3 product renders (chips, mango drink, hair oil). Other islands stay "your-company", never an invented customer. WhatsApp is marked "soon", as in PRODUCT.md.

Mock-up slips: garbled pack labels, and "Distributur".

L6 Plans, close and footer

Three plans described by scope, then the ending: the same town at dusk, the carton standing empty because the job is done.

Rules
No prices are invented: every plan says "Prices on request". The footer carries the staff console link and the fictional-data line.

In the build: no glow around the buttons. v3 rules out halos.

console.smartclearance.com

Where Smart-Clearance staff set up and run each client's workspace. A client's agents are laid out as the stops they work, with the human approval as a locked amber gate. Setting up a client means deciding how far each agent may go before a person says yes.

C1 Staff sign-in

A Google account at smartclearance.com, then a passkey. Client teams are pointed to their own workspace address.

Rule
Platform sign-in is separate from every client workspace, and carries no client's branding.

C2 Overview

Batches on the move as trackers rather than KPI tiles, what is waiting for a person (amber), and today's agent runs.

Mock-up slips: the sidebar should mark Overview, and the date should read Sunday 4 October.

C3 Clients

Munchly is the only client: domain, plan, status, distributors, SKUs, agents and money recovered. The empty state below starts the next one.

C4 Munchly › Agents Chosen

The heart of the console. Each agent's job, schedule and autonomy, in the order they work; the approval gate between deciding and doing.

Interaction
Changing an agent's autonomy re-tints its stop and writes an audit line. The panel holds the selected agent's limits and last run.
Rule
The gate can't be switched off for any client. The build removes the control the mock-up drew on it.

C5 Supply chain

How Munchly's stock moves, as set up: the chain, the six profile answers, and each distributor with its one-time permission.

Mock-up slip: the tab underline should sit on Supply chain.

C6 People

Everyone in Munchly's workspace with their role, sign-in method, access and status; invited partners in grey; an invite panel that states the sign-in rule.

Mock-up slip: the first tab label repeats "People".

C7 New client

Seven steps: company, workspace, supply chain, exits, agents, people, review. The supply-chain answers decide which exits and agents switch on, and the summary says so in plain words.

Rule
Every new workspace starts with the approval gate on.

C8 On a phone

The tracker runs full width; the selected agent's limits rise in a bottom sheet.

When you say go

  • Landing page, matched to the mock-ups. The approved hero becomes a measured spec; the town is regenerated at full resolution, and the first screen is checked against the mock-up before the other sections, the motion and the phone and dark versions follow.
  • Console, built from the v3 components. Munchly is seeded from the app's own data; every change is saved in the browser and writes an audit line.
  • Both pages join the accessibility suite at zero WCAG 2.2 AA violations in five viewports, and are published to Claude Design and pinned to a commit, like the demo and the app.
