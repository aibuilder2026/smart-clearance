Five grounds for ten agents

The workspace and console sign-ins have the agents' mesh, but almost nothing behind it. Each frame below adds one layer under the mesh. The mesh and the card stay as they are. These are quick sketches drawn in code to show the idea, not finished art. The painted ones (2 and 3) would be Qwen plates in the real build.

The batch walks the nine stops; the agent at work lights up.

The map under the agents

recommended · SVG

The guided demo's plate becomes the ground: dotted grid, city blocks and the river. Each agent stands on its real place, and the block under the agent at work lights up. At night the blocks glow like lit windows.
