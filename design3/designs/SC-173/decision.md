# SC-173 · Papers as evidence: the decision

## The request (11 Oct 2026)

1. "Can we somehow track receipts given to Staff sale, they will be needed for paperwork evidence - Distributor will be
   needed to upload a separate consolidated receipts for the staff sale (UPI payment IDs will do as part of the demo)."
2. The Certificate of Destruction to carry, as its audit trail: a detailed description, batch numbers and exact quantity
   and total weight; the reason (product expiry, contamination, court-ordered seizure); the approved environmental method
   (CPCB-compliant incineration or sanitary landfill); the precise date, time and authorised facility location; the
   signatures of the supervising officer and two independent witnesses. "The signatures of officers and witnesses can be
   faked."
3. "I see none of the GST invoices, credit note and price support note has signatures."

"I need old past batches and current batches to be fixed with above three requirements and then local and production
storage and database be synced."

## The options (board: `SC-173 design review.html`, app v3)

**Part 1 · the staff sale's consolidated receipt** (the Mango Drink at Lakshmi Agencies, 150 packs at ₹8)

- A · With the count (recommended): the receipt attached on the same card as the count, read by Vision; the sale
  records once the payments match.
- B · A step of its own: the count as today; the receipt follows as the distributor's next step, and the papers wait.
- C · The payments are the count: the UPI app's statement uploaded, the Data agent lists the payments, the ticks make
  the count, and Paperwork lays out a receipt voucher.

**Part 2 · the certificate of destruction** (MF-2407-111, OCE/DC/26-27/0217)

- A · One page, numbered (recommended): each mandatory item a numbered section, the three signatures at its foot.
- B · The certificate and its log: a summary page with the signatures, then the destruction log with times and photos.

In both: the agency's record gains its facility, coordinates, method and officer; the destruction screen gains the
time window and two independent witnesses.

**Part 3 · a signature on every paper**

- A · Ink and the firm's stamp (recommended): the specimen signature in ink over the firm's rubber stamp.
- B · Digitally signed: a DSC box on the GST papers and credit notes; ink on the certificate.
