A vast municipal landfill on the edge of an Indian city at dusk, a low wide shot from the foot of a fresh heap: a plain white dump truck with no markings has just tipped its load, and a fresh slide of brown cartons, many still sealed, crushed small orange drink cartons and spilled food packets pours down the heap toward the camera; further back a yellow tracked excavator pushes waste with its bucket raised; a flock of black kites circles in a hazy orange evening sky; thin grey smoke rises from the far end of the dump and drifts across the light; dust hangs in the low warm sunlight; sombre, cinematic, muted earthy palette with warm orange light.
