1 · The tour on the table

As the town's tour

The whole table stays in view; the camera leans a little toward the agent at work (1.35×), a small card opens on a stem beside it with a line that fills while its stop holds, and a caption at the foot carries the steps and Pause. 1.4 s an agent, 1.8 s on the yes; about 16 s in all.

Why

  • The closest to the landing page as it is: the agents at their posts, the whole business in one picture.
  • Calm: the picture barely moves; the cards do the telling.

Trade-offs

  • The cards are small, so what each agent is doing is a sentence, not the work itself.
  • The agents are at the edges of the picture, not at its centre.

2 · In focus

RecommendedAgents centred, at work

One agent at a time in a large card at the centre of the scene: its icon, name, post, what it did, and what it is doing live inside the card (the Watcher's gates and 1,360; the Valuer's five prices; the Router's split; the yes with ₹21,770 and the amber button; Outreach's Hindi offer; the Lister's lot; the Negotiator's counter; Paperwork's documents; Impact's kilos), with a rail of all eleven to jump between. Behind it the table closes in on the agent's post (1.6×) and recedes a little, so the agent is in focus. Same pace, Pause.

Why

  • What was asked for in so many words: the agents centred and in focus, showing what is happening.
  • Each card carries the agent's real work, not a caption: the gates, the prices, the split, the offer, the counter, the documents, the kilos.
  • The rail lets a visitor jump to any agent; Pause holds the clock.

Trade-offs

  • The table is seen in pieces while the tour runs; it rests on the whole table before and after.
  • About 16 s of motion in the section, with Pause (WCAG 2.2.2).

A · The film

Shopify's hero, literally

The miniature business comes alive on film: the forklift rolls out of the godown, the trucks move, the people walk, the painted road brightens once. Over it, enormous light type whose last word turns once through "buyer, shelf, invoice, ledger line" and rests on "chance". The film runs 8 s, so it carries Pause. Below, Shopify's spine: the statement, four chapters, the ledger, the exits, the workspace, plans, the close.

Why

  • The most cinematic, and the closest port of Shopify's page.
  • The chapters carry the whole story in order, each with the real card.
  • The lighter display weight (Bricolage 520) tests Shopify's airiness on our type.

Trade-offs

  • Two clips (720p day, 540p night) on the first paint; the plates stand in until they load.
  • The hero shows the world, not the mechanism; the yes is a chapter down the page.
  • The lighter weight departs from DESIGN.md (720 to 780): an open question.

The build would touch

  • site/site.jsx: the hero, statement, chapters, ledger, exits strip, demo pill.
  • site/assets/media/: the two clips, with sidecars.
  • The port: Hero.svelte, new Statement, Chapter, Ledger, ExitsRow, DemoPill; Town.svelte retired.

B · Islands in the sky

The journey is the page

The business as six islands floating in a sky, joined by the painted road: the maker's factory, the distributor's godown, the kirana lane, a buyer's warehouse, a food bank and, farthest and smallest, the landfill. The copy stands in the band of cloud at the foot. Scrolling drives the stage through six stops: Stocked, At risk, Priced and split, One yes, Sold, Settled. Each lights its island and opens its card with the figure and the agents; during Sold the packs run the road as dots. A rail of the stops at the top left; a progress bar on phones.

Why

  • The journey told most completely, stop by stop, in one picture: the most surreal of the three.
  • Under the visitor's hand throughout: no autoplay, no video.
  • The same stage on every device, panned on phones.

Trade-offs

  • About six screens of scroll before the rest of the page; some visitors scroll through without reading.
  • Pins and cards over a busy picture need the wash and the glass to stay legible.
  • Close in kind to the town hero the maintainer has iterated three times.

The build would touch

  • site/site.jsx: the sky stage replaces the town; the statement, ledger, exits strip, demo pill.
  • site/assets/plates/: islands-sky.webp by day and night, with sidecars.
  • The port: Sky.svelte on motion's scroll values; Town.svelte retired.

C · One yes

RecommendedThe human moment

Shopify's real person at a kitchen counter becomes ours: 09:00, chai, a hand holding a phone over the table, and on the table the business in miniature. The plan is lifted off the phone as the real approve sheet, tied to the screen that shows the same sheet. The Approve button is live: tapping it places the plan, streams the packs out of the sheet to the kiranas and the buyer's truck, lights the agents as they work, and rolls the swing. Replay. Below: the agents' day as one line from 09:00 to the ledger, the exits, the ledger, the workspace, plans, the close.

Why

  • It proves the mechanism in the first viewport: the money on screen, one tap, the agents at work. What a visitor remembers is "I pressed Approve and watched the packs go".
  • The closest in spirit to Shopify's hero, a real human moment, in our world: surreal in scale, calm in colour.
  • Honest on every device: the plan is a phone screen, so on a phone it is the screen. No video, no scroll hijack.

Trade-offs

  • The lifted sheet must stay in the clear part of the picture at every size: held at 1440 and 390, to be checked at 1366 × 768 and 820 × 1180 in the build.
  • A visitor may try to tap the phone itself; the sheet is tied to it with a line and the phone shows the same sheet.
  • The hand is a render; the night plate is an edit of the day plate so the geometry holds.

The build would touch

  • site/site.jsx: the table hero with the live sheet and the stream; the statement, the agents' day, the exits strip, the ledger, the demo pill.
  • site/assets/plates/: table.webp by day and night, with sidecars.
  • The port: Yes.svelte and Day.svelte; the approve sheet shared with the app's; Town.svelte retired.
