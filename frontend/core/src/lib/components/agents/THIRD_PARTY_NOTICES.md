## ThreeUI Community

The agents at work behind the sign-ins (`AgentStage.svelte` and this folder's `mesh.ts` and `AgentField.svelte`, SC-195,
round 2, option A; design3's `AgentStage` in `design3/system/product.jsx`) adapt ThreeUI's
constellation field, from its free Community edition: `lib-dist/shaders/neuform-isolated/sources/constellation-field.html.js`
(the drifting field, its links by distance and their ramp, and the links drawn to the pointer).

Source: [`@designcodeio/threeui`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@designcodeio/threeui) 1.2.0 ([MengTo/threeui](https://github.com/MengTo/threeui), catalog commit `68802d5`, [threeui.com](https://threeui.com)).

It is redrawn in design system v3: the system's inks, no halos beyond the aura, eleven of its nodes named for the
platform's agents and the one person, and the walk of a batch through the nine stops drawn over it. It loops under its
own Pause and Play (WCAG 2.2.2) and is drawn at rest under reduced motion. No ThreeUI Pro or Beta source, and none of its
fonts or assets, is used. design3 carries the same notice beside its copy (`design3/system/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).
